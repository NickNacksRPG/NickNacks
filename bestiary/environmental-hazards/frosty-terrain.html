Frosty Terrain

Adventures bring creatures to all kinds of places, including those in the far northern reaches of the world. This should help you deal with things like snow and ice peppering or covering your battles.

Snow
Light snow usually not too cumbersome. To an average person it doesn't even submerge their shoes. Light snow only inflicts movement penalties on creatures who are small or smaller, who treat it as difficult terrain.

Heavy Snow
Heavy snow is very cumbersome. Usually at least a foot deep, heavy snow counts as difficult terrain unless you are huge or larger. Creatures who are small enough can Hide inside of snow by burrowing inside it.
