Cookiefication Su curse - With a touch, turn a creature into a sweet treat version of themselves (fort negates). Creatures with bite attacks gain a +4 bonus to attack against cookie creatures. A grandma can make a bite attack as an instant action against a creature that gets cookiefied.
Summon Ally (companion) - 2 Wrinklers
Grandma's Cookies - A grandma usually puts on a facade as some friendly old widow that has an eye for baked goods. She makes all kinds of cookies for those that live nearby. After 2 weeks of eating grandma's cookies regularly, she can turn a creature into an ally, as though using the Order 66 technique, but only in her true form. She can make this transformation as part of the action to trigger the takeover.
Any creature she activates this ability on takes a more eldritch form, looking fleshier than usual. They share a grandma's teamwork feats and gain the Tentacles for Arms technique at will.
