River Spirit

A soft spoken young woman swims around a lake, offering you a spot amongst those having fun inside. The glamer removed once you hop in, you see a pile of corpses reaching to grab you.

Level 1 Medium Undead/Fey Boss

AC 16
HP 52
Poise 25

Resistance - Necrotic
Recovery
Bound - River spirits are bound to the body of water they perished in and cannot be forcefully removed from it. They also cannot willingly leave it.
Washed Away recharge elusive - As a swift action, a river spirit can cause a wave of water to push itself or an ally within 30 ft. It pushes up to 30 feet in a straight line.
Beneath the Waves - As long as its attacker is not immersed in water, gains resistance to ALL damage from said creature.

Pool Party glamer - A river spirit is surrounded by the victims it kills. From outside of the water the victims and the spirit appear as though they have taken on aquatic forms and are enjoying life beneath the surface of the water.

For creatures that are inside of the water, the skeletal victims reach for them and pull them under. The victims cannot be attacked. They deal 17 poise damage each turn persistent. If poise broken, the creature is grappled. A creature can only hold their breath for half the usual time (typically 2 rounds, or 4 with +3 STR) and starts drowning if they are grappled for too long.

Boss Actions - Usable 1/turn
Jig is Up (recharge) companion- Once the jig is up and creatures realize the true nature of the river spirit, she lets 2 skeletal victims walk free, functioning as skeletons. They gain the river spirit's +Teamwork Feats, a 40 ft swim speed and a +4 bonus to grapple attacks.
Rattle 'em Boys - Command all companion skeletons to make a Simple Attack.
Water's Caress elusive - Move up to 20 feet. If there is a grappled creature within reach during this movement, automatically land a caress attack.

Weaknesses/Deep Lore

Temptation charm - A river spirit has an aura that compels creature to approach the water's edge (will negates). Each turn a creature stands next to the water, they are unknowingly drained 4 as it caresses them. The effects of the drain are not apparent to onlookers or the creature until the river spirits hold is broken. Each turn a tempted creature may attempt a new will save to realize what is happening

If creatures that are not tempted happen to pass a +Religion check, they automatically learn of this quality.
Weakness - Crush 2
