Cheez

A duo of cheesy ladies rest on a slice of floating pizza. They work in tandem, encouraging and enhancing each other.

Level 6 Medium Elemental

Defense Traits

AC 20
HP 79
Poise 27
Saves Fort +8, Refl +8, Will +8

Resistance - Fire
Amorphous - +13
Never Alone - Cannot be flanked
Soggy recharge - As an instant action, a cheezette can absorb a water effect, as though it was dispelled. It can do this once an hour.

Offense Traits (DC 18)

Dangerously Cheesy (Fire) +8
4d8+3 (21) +Sizzle

Innate Techniques - Blazing Palm, Flame Cleanse Me, Gooball, Splatter, Dispel Magic
1/day - Mirror Images, To the Rescue!, Blazing Beam, Backfire

Sizzle fire - Some of the hot cheese is left behind by a cheezettes touch. It is applied on hit, and functions exactly as ablaze.
Sisters in Arms - Cheezette functions more like a two-headed creature, so it still only gets 1 turn, but the two assist each other in combat. At start of turn, gain 1 benefit for 1 round.

  • Gain Advantage on Attack
  • Gain advantage on skill checks
  • Gain advantage on saving throws
  • Gain advantage on CL checks
    Dangerously Cheesy (fire) - When creature melee attacks a cheezete, the weapon they use to attack gets stuck unless they succeed on brute force to pull it out. If they fail and they don’t let go of the weapon, it is heated up and they take 3d6 (10) fire damage. It can wield any weapons that are left behind in it.

If the attack is a projectile weapon, they can spit it back out at their enemy, making an attack with the same range as an immediate action.
Summon Ally (Recharge) - 1 Big Cat (cheetah variant). Only 1 can be summoned at once.

Weaknesses/Deep Lore

Topping Overload - For every weapon stuck inside of it past the first, a cheezette has its speed lowered by 5. It can remove a stuck weapon from itself as a swift action. Stuck ammunition does not lower its speed.
Weakness - Poison 3

Ability Scores - STR +2, DEX +2, LUK +3, INT +1, WIS +1, CHA +1
Languages - Primordial
Deific Boons - Gift of Giving
Speed - 20 Land Speed, 40 Hover
Feats - Point Blank Master, Divine Interference, Toughness
Skills - +9 👀+Perception, +11 💉+Medicine, +9 +Persuasion, +9 🤔+Insight
