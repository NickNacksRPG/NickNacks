(7) Jad

This is gonna hurt...

Level 10 Massive Elemental boss Unique

Defense Traits

AC 26 +4 Molten Shield
HP 240
Poise 55
Saves Fort +17, Refl +12, Will +12

Resistance - Fire
Amorphous - +14
Molten Shield - Jad is immune to any effect that decreases his speed or limits his actions. Gain +4 AC and resistance to all damage. Cannot fall below half +HP while this shield persists.
Massive - Jad's movement is Elusive against creatures that are medium or smaller

Offense Traits (DC 24)

Unarmed (C) +18
2d20+6 (no average (:<) +Overwhelming Force

Here Comes Jad! - Jad falls from the ceiling of the battlefield and crashes into the center of the arena. All creatures inside are instantly set to 1 poise.
Overwhelming Force - On-hit Jad's bite deals an equal amount of poise damage. If this poise breaks, the creature is tossed 60 feet directly away from him.
Fire Storm fire persistent - Jad causes many pillars of flame to appear around the battlefield, functioning as the Pillar of Flame technique with the following changes: It appears underneath each enemy in combat, regardless of distance. They do not require concentration and they persist until Jad is defeated. They deal 2d20 damage (reflex halves).

Boss Sequence - The Jad fight is unique in that it is intended to have "phases." It is intended to be an incredibly long and difficult fight where the turns proceed as follows.
1st Turn - Jad unleashes a powerful volley of attacks as a standard action (see below).
2nd Turn - Jad gets real hungry and moves towards the nearest source of lava, unleashing a fire storm and summoning a sequence of enemies reminiscent of previous waves.
3rd Turn - Jad recharges his assault and loses his molten shield for 1 round while he guzzles some nearby molten rocks (standard action).
This sequence will repeat itself until Jad gets below 120 HP.
Below Half - Jad will summon 4 (6) Servant of Jad. In 4 random directions 30 ft away from him that act after the current turn.

Jad's Assault
Volcanic Surge - The lava surrounding the battlefield closes in, covering the outer 10 ft of the battlefield. Pick a random direction. A 30 ft line in the ground extending from this outer edge cracks and fills with lava under the nearest creature, hitting them with partial submersion in 🌋lava (reflex negates).
Crush earth - Jad slams the ground and causes a stalactite to crack on the ceiling above each enemy, leaving a bunch of rocks on the ground beneath as an indicator for what is to come. At the end of the creature's next turn, a huge (size category, as if it were a huge creature) falls down upon them, dealing 2d20 crush damage. Creatures within 20 ft of the impact zone (except Jad) take 2d20 poise damage, knocking down any that are poise broken.
This effect also leaves behind a stalactite that alters the terrain, providing cover and a place to get ⛰high ground, and a place to escape the lava if it rises high enough.
Time's Ticking (fire) - Jad spits a fire blast that slowly moves towards the nearest creature. At the end of the creatures next turn, it homes in on their location and explodes in their space, dealing 2d20 fire damage in a 20 ft AoE (reflex halves).
If the creature somehow becomes untargetable, the next closest enemy is chosen instead.
Earthquake - All enemies take 2d20 poise damage. Poise broken creatures are Knocked Down.

Weaknesses/Deep Lore

Wet Weakness - Takes a -4 penalty on saves against effects with the water tag. Any attacks with the water tag have advantage against this creature.
Clumsy Titan - Jad cannot make AoOs against creatures of medium size or smaller.
Focus - Jad will prefer targeting creatures without a form of cover with his attacks.

Other Traits

Ability Scores - STR +6, DEX +0, LUK +0, INT -2, WIS +0, CHA +2
Languages - Primordial (cannot speak)
Lava Walker - Jad can move through 🌋lava, even if it is liquid, without harm to itself. Ignores difficult terrain that is lava-based.
Speed - 40 Land Speed
Feats - Pack Tactics, Skill Focus
Skills - +12 (adv) 👀+Perception, +26 (adv) 🏊+Athletics
