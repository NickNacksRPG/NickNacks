Chrimbus Killer

A weapon rolls in on treads made to appear as a malicious mechanical 🎅Santa. Its wide array of weapons rev up while lets out laugh as joyful as it is creepy.

Level 7 Huge Construct Boss

Defense Traits

AC 21
HP 130
Poise 40
Saves Fort +7, Refl +10, Will +8

Keep It Cool - Whenever a Chrimbus Killer is affected by an effect that creates a great deal of water or ice, it creates a cloud of 🌫Fog in its space. This fog provides Partial Concealment until it disperses as a result of some other effect or wind.
Recovery
Intelligent Design - A Chrimbus Killer is immune to any damage or status effect inflicted by its own attacks (like getting caught in its missile AoE).

Offense Traits (DC 20)

Arm Cannon (P) +10 🏹Long
d10+3 (8)

Multiattack - Make 3 arm cannon attacks

Innate Techniques - Laser Pointer
1/day - Rain of Arrows, Gravity Bow, Snatch, Self-Destruct

Deathrattle - Fire all remaining missiles at random enemies within range.
Bonus Points - Whenever a Chrimbus Killer moves, it automatically attempts to crush any creature it moves through. The target creature can choose to AoO or make a reflex save to avoid being crushed. The creature becomes Drained 7 (reflex negates). Any creatures it runs over get deposited behind it.
Gift Grenade - As a swift action, a Chrimbus Killer can drop off a gift in an adjacent space. At the start of its next turn, or when a creature opens/interacts with it, it explodes violently, dealing 5d6 (17) crush and poise damage to all creatures within 5 ft.

Boss Actions - Can do 2 of the following actions each turn.
Fire the Missiles Recharge - When another creature ends their turn, fire a single missile at a creature within 60 ft. It deals 7d8 (28) fire damage in a 10 ft radius (reflex halves) and leaves flames on the ground that set creatures Ablaze if they end their turn inside of them. Chrimbus Killers come loaded with 5 missiles.
Unload - When another creature ends their turn, the Chrimbus killer revs up its gun and sprays a 30 ft cone of bullets, dealing 7d8 (28) pierce damage (reflex halves). If a creature ends its turn in the radius, it takes this damage again. It cannot use its arm cannon attack on its next turn.
Lock On - When a creature damages the Chrimbus Killer, it can choose to laser focus on it. It gets Advantage on attack rolls against it and the creature has disadvantage on saving throws against the Chrimbus Killer's offensive abilities for 1 round.

Weaknesses/Deep Lore

Turn Radius - A Chrimbus Killer cannot move in more than one direction at once. It must choose a direction and move directly in that direction, or diagonally towards that direction.
No Brakes - When a Chrimbus Killer is subject to Forced Movement, it moves an additional 10 ft.

Other Traits

Ability Scores - STR +0, DEX +3, LUK +3, INT -1, WIS -1, CHA +0
Languages - Common, Sylvan
Speed - 20 Land Speed, Ignores Difficult Terrain
Feats - Snap Shot, Point Blank Master, Clustered Shots, Butterfly Sting
Skills - +8 👀+Perception, +9 🏊+Athletics, +12 +Stealth
