Automaton

A humanoid robot stands a few heads taller than a man with oversized arms. You see it stop the thief that rushes out of the town square by launching one of its arms as a projectile and pinning them.

Level 2 Large Construct

Defense Traits

AC 16
HP 40
Poise 25
Saves Fort +5, Refl +4, Will +2 +4 vs Mind-Affecting

Resistance - Poison, Psychic, Force

Offense Traits (DC 14)

Unarmed (P) +5 Melee or Thrown, +4 Grapple
2d6+3 (10) +2 zap Overcharged

Multiattack - Make 2 unarmed attacks

Innate Techniques - Pin Down

Rocket Fists - An automaton can launch its unarmed strikes as thrown attacks. They magnetize back immediately unless they are grabbed out of the air or they use their Pin Down technique.
Special Attack: Rocket Punch - Make a thrown weapon attack using unarmed damage. On-hit make a drag attack. If the drag is successful, the creature is pulled adjacent.
Special Attack: Power Fist - On-hit stagger your target for one round (fort negates). If after this attack is resolved the target is defeated, it flies 1,000 ft into the air and gently lands on the ground one minute later. With a low roof, the creature is launched less high, but for just as long.
Overdrive zap Recharge - As a swift action, an automaton can enter overdrive, giving it the benefits of Static Field for 1 turn. On the following turn it must recharge, and reduces its speed to 15. If it ever uses the discharge option of static field, overdrive cannot be used again until rest.

Weaknesses/Deep Lore

Memo - When a duedette is Defeated, a bird erupts from their body that flies off to deliver a message to its superior of its demise. Can be easily intercepted with most forms of ranged attacks within 3 rounds. Past that it usually flies to high and too far for most creatures to catch it.
Just Business - A duedette is forbidden from attacking creatures unprovoked, as they they were under the effects of Sanctuary. They are often permitted to use force when collecting debts, but will typically use it as a last resort. They can still fight in self-defense, however.

Other Traits

Ability Scores - STR +0, DEX +3, LUK +0, INT +1, WIS +3, CHA +0
Languages - Common. By listening to someone speak a language, an automaton can slowly learn how to speak a language over the course of 1 minute.
Scanning Database - As a FRA, an automaton can make any kind of knowledge check using its engineering bonus, but with Disadvantage.
Speed - 25 Land Speed
Feats - Ricochet Toss, Point Blank Master
Skills - +4 👀+Perception, +7 🏊+Athletics, +8 🤖Engineering
