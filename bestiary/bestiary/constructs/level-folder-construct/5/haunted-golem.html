Haunted Golem

You see a statue whose color has faded away with time. Suddenly your sword comes out of its sheathe and starts to swing at you on its own. Once you grab it out of the air, the statue animates and flings you into the air.

Level 5 Large Construct/Undead

Offense Traits (DC 17)

Unarmed (C) +8 +8 Disarm, +4 MV
2d6+3 (10)

Innate Techniques (CL 7) - Icicle, Sky Uppercut (U3), Life Drinker, Never Lucky (U3), Chill Metal, Sanctuary, Barrier, Mud Buddy
1/day - Earth Shatter, Haunted, Fake Out, Foe Toss

Possess Object Su Subtle Recharge - While hiding in plain sight, a haunted golem can covertly possess an item within 30 ft, giving the item a Hover speed of 30 and the ability to communicate telepathically. As a standard action, the golem can make a Melee Attack with the object, dealing either the weapon damage or its unarmed damage, whichever is higher. It can use the weapons special attacks, but otherwise no other types of special attacks.

The possessed object has Poise equal to the haunted golems. When disarmed, the animating force in the weapon disappears and this ability ends. The creature that successfully disarmed the weapon can choose to wield the weapon.
Protector - Whenever an allied Minion is damaged, a haunted golem can choose to move its speed to Threaten the enemy that did the damage. If they already threatened them, they can make a Simple Attack against them.

Weaknesses/Deep Lore

Protector of the Innocent - A haunted golem can intuitively tell when a creature is a minion. It will not do anything that could cause +HP damage to such creatures.
