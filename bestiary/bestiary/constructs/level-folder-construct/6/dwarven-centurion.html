Dwarven Centurion

The pinnacle of dwarven engineering designed to deal death made in the image of its creators. It lumbers forward and gyrates its limbs with extreme precision.

Level 6 Large Construct

AC 19
HP 102
Poise 40
Saves Fort +10, Refl +8, Will +6 +4 vs Mind-Affecting

Resistance - Slash, Pierce, Fire
Superheated - Creatures have disadvantage on drag and grapple attacks against a centurion that has activated one of its Supercharge attacks unless they have resistance or immunity to fire/heat.

Ballista Arm - Functions as the siege weapon, but it reloads automatically, requiring 1 round of downtime between shots. As a FRA, it can reload and fire its ballista (or fire then reload). Centurions come with 4 bolts loaded, requiring 2 turns of manual restocking (or aid from multiple allies) to get a new supply.
Special Attack: Supercharge Recharge - Gain +4 to an attack roll. Recharges after using Letting off Steam.
Letting off Steam (recharge) fire air water - Special: A dwarven centurion cannot use this ability until it has supercharged one of its other attacks.
Let out a cone of superheated steam in a 30 ft cone or a 10 ft radius around it, dealing 6d8 (27) fire damage and inflicting sickened 2 (fort halves).
