Insect Swarm

A bug lands on your arm and you swat it. Another 2, then 5, then 10. You turn and see a tsunami of insects washing over your campsite.

Level 4 Huge Beast Group

Defense Traits

AC 14
HP 60
Poise -
Saves +6, +7, +5

Poise Immunity - Group enemies do not have poise and cannot be poise broken.
Single Target Resistance - Resistance to any damage that only affects a single creature, most common with attack rolls. Certain attacks like cleave can ignore this resistance, but you can only attack the swarm with such moves.

Single target effects that deal no damage have no effect.

Offense Traits (DC 14)

Swarm Attack (P) 3d6 (10) +Distraction

Swarm Attack - A swarm does not threaten. These creatures cannot make attacks and must instead enter a creatures space to deal their damage automatically at the end of their turn. This allows them to damage multiple creatures if they are close together with a big enough swarm.
Distraction - When a creature takes damage from an insect swarm, they are overwhelmed by the sheer number of creatures. They are staggered for one round (fort negates).
THEY'RE IN MY EVERYTHING - While within a swarm, creatures are sickened 2 and have blurred vision. Exiting immediately ends both conditions.
Species - Bugs come in all shapes and sizes, pick one.
Scarab - Creatures hurt by swarm attack also start bleeding for d10 damage (fort negates).
Ant - Ants crawl into every nook and cranny and never let go. Their swarm damage happens again at the end of the creatures turn if not hit with AoE, submerged in water, etc. Lose fly speed, gain 30 ft climb.
Bee Poison - Deals an additional 2d6 poison damage with swarm attack (fort negates).
Locust - Always focuses plant creatures first. Deal double damage to plant creatures.
Spider - Gains the Venomous quality, applied on swarm attack. Lose fly speed, gain 30 ft climb.

Weaknesses/Deep Lore

Baited - While piranhas do attack living creatures, they prefer easier prey. If a fresh dead carcass is within range of their blood scent, piranhas will tear it to shreds before moving on. Medium or small creatures distract for one turn. Anything smaller only uses their movement. Anything larger than medium distracts for 1 more turn per size category.
AoE Vulnerability - Takes 50% more dmg from any AoE effect.

Other Traits

Ability Scores - STR +0, DEX +3, LUK +0, INT -3, WIS +1, CHA +0
Deific Boons - Animal Instincts
Senses - Scent 10 ft or blood scent (vs bleeding creatures, 100 ft)
Speed - 5 Land Speed, 40 Swim
Signature Skill - one eye open, quick scan
Feats - Signature Skill
Skills - +5 (+4 scent) 👀+Perception, +9 🏊+Athletics
