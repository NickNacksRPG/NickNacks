Big Cat

A rustling in the bushes and a low growl are the last thing you hear before a giant cat pounces from the underbrush, directly onto your neck.

Level 4 Large Beast

Defense Traits

AC 18
HP 60
Poise 32 DR 10 vs Grapple
Saves +7, +9, +5

Cat's Grace - Take 1 damage for every 10 ft fallen. Never subject to drain from falling.

Offense Traits (DC 16)

Bite (P) +7 Only usable against grappled creatures
3d8+3 (16) +Predatory instincts
Claw (S) +7
2d6+3 (10) +Predatory instincts

Multiattack - Make 2 claw attacks. One must be Grapple.

Innate Techniques - Roar, Pack Attack
1/day - Jaws of the Beast
Constant - Predatory Instincts

Special Attack: Bushwack - While hiding, a big cat can take the jump action and attack a creature, dealing an additional 2d8 (9) dmg. If big cat successfully hides, it can also grapple the creature as an instant action.
Rend - 2 claws, or a grapple and a claw (2d6)
Species - Big cats come in a variety of shapes and sizes.
Cheetah - Gain powerful charge. When it charges or dashes in a straight line, it can move up to 160 feet.
Lion - Can innately use the Warcry technique 1/day.
Tiger - Creatures grappled are also Knocked Down and held down.
Panther/Jaguar - Creatures grappled are also staggered.

Weaknesses/Deep Lore

Bath Time - Big cats will avoid water if they can get away with it and prefer attacking targets not in water. If made wet, a big cat will always spend their swift action to remove the wet condition with a violent shake.

Other Traits

Ability Scores - STR +3, DEX +3, LUK +0, INT -3, WIS +1, CHA +0
Deific Boons - Animal Instincts
Senses - Scent 5 ft, Nightvision
Speed - 45 Land Speed, Can Jump up to 30 ft
Favored Terrain - In its natural habitat, a big cat gets a +4 bonus to +Stealth checks.
Feats - Maneuver Versatility, Swift Grappler, Skill Focus
Skills - +7 (+4 scent) 👀+Perception, +9 🏊+Athletics, +9 (+4, adv) +Stealth
