Harambe

This slow paced ape walks on its knuckles before realizing your presence. It rears up on its hind legs to show off its massive size while it screeches and swings its arms.

Level 2 Large Beast

AC 17
HP 40
Poise 25
Saves +3, +7, +3 Advantage vs fear

Freedom - At the start of the eagle's turn, it loses the entangled and entombed conditions. It is freed from a grapple.
Let Freedom Ring Su Recharge - As a standard action, an eagle can cry out to free all creatures within 100 ft. Any creature that is grappled, entangled or entombed automatically loses the condition and becomes immune to these effects for 1 round.
Flyby Attack - An eagle's movement is elusive against targets it has attacked this round.

Weaknesses/Deep Lore

Never Forget - Harambe have weakness 2 to Gun attacks, and are shaken 1 on hit by them.
Dominance Established - Harambe are often led by an elder male who calls the shots and protects the rest of the troop. In neutral scenarios where harambe encounter other creatures, they will attempt to intimidate them to assess their foes. By succeeding on an opposed 😱+Intimidation check, a creature can usually dissuade harambe from engaging.
