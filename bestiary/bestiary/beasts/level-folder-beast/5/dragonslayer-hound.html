Dragonslayer Hound

Clad in a bone-like exoskeleton armor, this dog sniffs you up and down before moving onto the next capture. It howls and the man next to your transforms into a dragon. The hound pounces on its quarry.

Level 5 Medium Beast

HP 60

Saves Fort +6, Refl +10, Will +8

Evasion

Bite (P) +8 +4 vs 🐲Dragons
2d8+3 (12) +Fire Fang

Multiattack - Whenever a dragonslayer hound takes fire damage, it gains access to this multiattack on its next turn.
Make 3 bite attacks.

Innate Techniques - Boiling Blood, Pack Attack, Jaws of the Beast
1/day - Blazing Beam, Feast (U3), Down to Earth (U3)
Constant - Enhanced Jump

Fire Fang Fire - On-hit with a bite attack, the foe is set Ablaze (reflex negates).
Dragonslayer - A dragonslayer hound has very specialized combat training. It gets a +4 bonus to attack against 🐲Dragons, including dragon adjacent creatures or creatures with a draconic affinity.
Flash Fire - A dragonslayer hound can redirect Fire effects, similar to how the Lightning Rod technique functions as an immediate action if the effect originates within 60 ft of it. Targeted spells such as Fire Blast are now centered on the hound while AoE spells like are Fire and Brimstone are directed towards the hound instead of their intended direction.

Against dragon a breath weapon specifically, a dragonslayer hound can use this ability as an instant action. When it does so, it turns the effect into a line attack directed towards itself, regardless of the original direction and shape of the ability.

Weaknesses/Deep Lore

Wet Dog - A dragonslayer hound cannot use its Fire Fang ability while Wet or Frosty.

Other Traits

Ability Scores - STR +1, DEX +3, LUK +0, INT -3, WIS +3, CHA +0
Deific Boons - Animal Instincts
Senses - Scent 15 ft
Speed - 45 Land Speed
Signature Skill - advanced tracking
Swift Tracker - Dragonslayer hounds can always track while moving at full speed. If the hound's quarry is faster than them, they can match their speed while tracking.
Find Quarry - Dragonslayer hounds can always smell when a creature is a dragon (as per its Dragonslayer ability), even if it is under the effect of a Polymorph. As a standard action, a dragonslayer hound can bark at a polymorphed dragon within 15 ft to automatically transform it back to its true form.
