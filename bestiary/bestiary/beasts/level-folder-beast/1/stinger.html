Stinger

A massive insect with an even more massive stingers flies charges at you, the drone of its massive wings disorienting and frustrating you as the rest of the colony pump you with venom.

Level 1 Small Beast

Defense Traits

AC 17 +2 Drone
HP 30
Poise 18
Saves Fort +3, Refl +5, Will +2

Offense Traits (DC 13)

Sting (P) +3 +2 Charge
2d6+2 (9) +Venom

Multiattack - Can only be used against a creature with 0 speed.
Make 2 sting attacks.

Venom Recharge - Has a limitation that its venom can only be applied once per minute.
Drone - A stinger makes a constant loud buzzing sound as it flies. It can choose to fly around an adjacent creature's head to bother it and taunt it for 1 round (will negates). A stinger gets +2 AC any turn it drones. Cannot use if it cannot fly.
Special Attack - Lunge
Special Attack: Skewer - Pierce through a creature, dealing an additional d6 (3) damage and entangling the stinger and the target On-hit until the start of the stingers next turn.
Powerful Charge

Weaknesses/Deep Lore

Swat - A stinger is also grounded for 1 turn in addition to any other effect from being poise broken. When grounded, a stinger has 13 AC.

Other Traits

Ability Scores - STR +2, DEX +2, LUK +0, INT -3, WIS +1, CHA +0
Speed - 10 Land Speed, 30 Fly
Senses - Nightvision, Scent (10 ft)
Deific Boons - Animal Instincts
Feats - Skill Focus
Skills - +4 (adv) 👀+Perception, +5 (adv) ⚖+Acrobatics
