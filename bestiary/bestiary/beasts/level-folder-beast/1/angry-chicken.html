Angry Chicken

This unassuming beast meanders about with its kin. Only when you bother it does it reveal its menacing aura and incredible power.

Level 1 Tiny Beast

Defense Traits

AC 13
HP 32
Poise 23 DR 2
Saves Fort +3 (+4 Enraged), Refl +3, Will +2

Offense Traits (DC 13)

Peck (P) +3 +2 Enraged
2d6+2 (9) +2 Enraged
New Life (C) +5 Thrown, Recharge
3d6+4 (14)

Multiattack - Must be Enraged. Make 2 peck attacks

Innate Techniques - Break Free, Reckless Charge

Enrage - While at full hp, the angry chicken is aggressive, but typically not threatening. After it takes any damage, it enrages, increasing its STR to +4, gains a multi-attack and the New Life and Chicken Scratch attacks.
Special Attack: Chicken Scratch - Flap up to a creatures face and peck at its eyes. On-hit apply blurred vision for 1 round.

Weaknesses/Deep Lore

Bed time - If an angry chicken is covered up, or otherwise subject to complete darkness, it must make a DC 10 will save or fall asleep and stop raging. If it is again exposed to light it awakens.

Other Traits

Ability Scores - STR +0, DEX +2, LUK +0, INT -3, WIS +1, CHA +0
Speed - 40 Land Speed, Glide
Deific Boons - Animal Instincts
Feats - Back to the Wall
Skills - +4 👀+Perception
