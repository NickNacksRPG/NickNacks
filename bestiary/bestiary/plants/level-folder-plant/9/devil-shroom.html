Devil Shroom

This mushroom has arms with clawed hands and horns that jut from its mushroom cap. It seems to ride a vortex of flame around and its more than happy to share.

Blaze It - All damage the devil shroom deals is automatically converted to fire damage.
Trailblazer - All spaces a devil shroom moves through with its hover speed are set on fire. Creatures moving through such spaces are set Ablaze (reflex negates).
Special Snowflake - A devil shroom benefits from the empowered version of its Ice Lance technique against creatures that are either Glowing or ablaze.
Devouring Flames - When using its Plasma Blade attack, a devil shroom can choose to inflict Bone Chilled instead of glowing.

Weaknesses/Deep Lore

Brakes - When Wet, the devil shroom cannot use its hover speed.
Sealed Soul - A devil shroom gains the Soul Cage 💀Medium ability but it can be used as an instant action at no cost. It manifests itself as a the flaming skull it uses to make attacks with its Plasma Blade. If that technique is ever dispelled, or the devil shroom ceases Concentration, the soul is freed and the devil shroom must capture a new soul to use its Plasma Blade.
The soul may choose to linger briefly to thank its rescuers and curse its warden.
