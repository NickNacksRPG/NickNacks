Town Guard

An armored warrior stands vigilant over a crown of people in a marketplace. They have a wide assortment of weapons and gaze at you warily as you pass by, sword arm resting on the hilt.

Stop Right There! Mind-Affecting - If a guard sees someone in the act of a crime, or is able to identify a known criminal with 🧠+Knowledge, they can shout at them as a standard action to briefly stop them in their tracks, reducing their speed by 30 for 1 round (will negates). A creature can only be affected by this 1/day, no matter how many guards shout.

Weaknesses/Deep Lore

Only Human - Guards have lives outside of protecting a town. As such they will typically not engage against creatures they successfully identify are level 6 or higher without someone else of a similar level commanding them.
