Molten Giant

The metal this giant wears glows red hot. The weapon glows even hotter. When you draw your weapons it only seems to put fuel on the fire that is their hair.

Level 5 Large Giant/Elemental

Defense Traits

AC 21 (heavy armor)
HP 70
Poise 32 DRA 1
Saves Fort +9, Refl +4, Will +9 +4 Earth

Resistance - Fire
Super Hot - Any creatures without fire resistance that maintains prolonged contact (such as drag or grapple) with a molten giant take 4 fire damage and are sickened 2 from the intense pain.

Offense Traits (DC 17)

2h Flail (C+Fire) +11 +4 MV
2d8+4+d6 (16) +Giant Slam, +5 Weapon Focus
Molten Rock (C+Fire) +11 Thrown
2d6+4+2d6 (18) +3d6 (10) Lava

Multiattack - Make 2 melee attacks

Innate Techniques - Awesome Blow, Foe Toss
1/day - Colossal Smash, Earth Shatter
Constant - Shockwave

Heat of Battle - At the start of a molten giants turn, it generates 2 Heat. Whenever a molten giant takes fire damage, it gains heat stacks equal to how much fire damage it took (after resistance). As a swift action, a molten giant can superheat its weapon, adding its heat stacks as additional fire damage on-hit, after which the heat dissipates until used again.
Molten Rock fire - By spending 2 heat as a standard action and with 2 hands free, a molten giant can dig its fingers into a stone surface and melt it enough to give it something to throw, or it can super heat a nearby rock of adequate size. The rock is a thrown weapon that deals 2d6+4 (11) crush and 2d6 (7) fire damage.

The creature also has molten rock cling to them, dealing 3d6 (10) damage at the start of their turn. This damage decreases by another d6 each turn. If the creature is cleaned or otherwise cooled then the damage stops.
Giant Slam - Many giants deal 10 poise damage on-hit to any creature smaller than them when they make melee attacks. If this breaks poise, the creature is either shoved or knocked down, giants choice.
Special Attacks - Wild Strike, Tenderize

Weaknesses/Deep Lore

Extinguish - Whenever a molten giant is made wet while they have heat, they negate it and instead lose all heat stacks. They also cannot gain heat for 1 round.

Other Traits

Ability Scores - STR +4, DEX -1, LUK +0, INT +1, WIS +2, CHA +0
Languages - Common, giant, primordial
Easy Fix - If a piece of metal equipment a molten giant wields becomes damaged, they can spend 1 Heat as a swift action to fix it.
Equipment - 1x Heavy Armor, 1x 2h flail,
Speed - 35 Land Speed
Feats - Skill Focus, Pet, Weapon Focus (crush), Maneuver Versatility
Skills - +9 👀+Perception, +9 (adv) 🤔+Insight, +9 🐾+Survival, +8 🍃+Nature, +11 🏊+Athletics, +13 🧑‍🌾+Profession (blacksmithing)
