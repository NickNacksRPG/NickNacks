Frost Giant Spirit

Perhaps filled with a little too much spirit, this ghost wanders frozen wastes chanting a sad song.

Level 5 Large Giant/Undead

Defense Traits

AC 18
HP 50
Poise 22
Saves Fort +5, Refl +8, Will +7

Resistance - Cold
Incorporeal
Lullaby Su - As long as the spirit takes no other action other than moving, it can sing and gain the effects of Sanctuary. Any other ally within 60 ft can gain this benefit with the same restrictions as long as they speak the giant language.

3d8+3 (16) +Frosty (if cold), +Sickened 1 (if necrotic)

Constant - Life Drinker

Pain sonic fear recharge - Special: A spirit must have taken damage from an enemy to activate this ability.
Shriek with immense volume. Creatures within the spirits natural reach take 6d6 (21) sonic damage. Creatures within 60 ft are also shaken 1 and deafened for 1 minute (will negates). If the creature was already shaken, they become frightened for 1 round.

Weaknesses/Deep Lore

Team Spirit? - A frost giant remembers its home team, and refuses to fight anyone it recognizes as a member or fan of its home team, even while charmed, possessed or otherwise controlled.
Song of Sorrow - Frost giant spirits are only formed as a result of being killed in battle and left to rot. As such, their songs they chant regularly are filled with great tragedy.
