G'nome

A pointed hat and thick white hair leading into a beard and mustache grace the head of this short creature. One of its allies tosses it across the battlefield and reveals the hat isn't for show.

Level 1 Small Fey

Defense Traits

AC 16
HP 30
Poise 26
Saves Fort +3, Refl +3, Will +2

Stick the Landing - When a gnome falls, they can choose to stick into the ground with their pointed hats to negate the fall damage as an instant action. Their speed is reduced by 20 and they cannot use their pointy feature for 1 round.

Offense Traits (DC 13)

Battle Ladder (C) +3 +4 MV, +4 GU, +2 disarm/dirty trick/drag
2d8+2 (11)

Innate Techniques - Boasting Taunt
1/day - Backstab, Pocket Sand, Kidnap

Snatch and Grab - After using +Sleight of Hand to steal something, or disarming an opponent, all of the gnomes actions become elusive against the target of their theft.
Pointy elusive - When a creature falls onto a space or jumps into a space within 10 ft of a gnome, the gnome can move to the space directly where the creature will land and use its pointed hat to damage them as an immediate action. The creature takes 2d6 (7) pierce damage and is made clumsy 1. They can choose any space adjacent to the gnome to land.
Sharp Aim - A gnome can spend a standard action to prepare and assume the position. Any other allied creature of at least medium size can throw a gnome in the position as though they were a thrown weapon with +2 attack (as though the gnome was aiding them), dealing 5d6 (17) piercing damage.

If a gnome is poise broken while assuming the position, its stance falters and it can no longer be thrown.

Weaknesses/Deep Lore

Gnome Toss - Gnomes are surprisingly aerodynamic and light. After grappling a gnome, you can choose to end your grapple to toss the gnome (🏊+Athletics roll) feet in a direction of your choosing. This also happens if you successfully shove, with the athletics roll determining the extra distance moved.

This happens instead of the usual effect of grapple and in addition to the effects of a shove.
One of a Kind - Gnomes love to steal things that normally come as a set, but only 1 of them, such as a pair of socks or several wheels on a vehicle. When presented with the opportunity to pilfer such an object, a gnome will do everything in their power to steal it. This could mean invading a home after hours, staking out a vehicle etc.

Other Traits

Ability Scores - STR +2, DEX +2, LUK +2, INT +0, WIS -1, CHA +2
Languages - Sylvan, Common
Equipment - 1 Battle Ladder (2h weapon)
Garden Gnome Su poly, true - Gnomes can transform into a lawn ornament that resembles a gnome. In this form, they can only move while not observed, unless the observers are gnomes. This effectively functions as Hide in Plain Sight.
Speed - 40 Land Speed
Deific Boons - enlightened
Feats - Maneuver Master, Maneuver Versatility, Gang Up
Skills - +2 👀+Perception, +5 +Stealth, +7 +Sleight of Hand, +5 +Deception, +3 +Cryptozoology
