Satyr

A mischievous pan flute playing creature with a humanoid torso and furry hooved legs prances about. Their magic music twists the nature of any magic affecting them with random results.

Level 4 Medium Fey

Defense Traits

AC 18
HP 52
Poise 25
Saves Fort +4, Refl +6, Will +5

Offense Traits (DC 16)

Satyr Shortbow (P) +6 Reload, BE
2d6+2 (9)

Multiattack - Make 2 ranged attacks

Innate Techniques (CL 5) - Reincarnate, Tentacles for Arms, Fraudgg, Flying Arrow (U2), WonkaS, Boasting Taunt
1/day - Mind Maze, Laser Pointer, Pin Down

Special Attack: Fairy Fire Su - Make a shortbow attack that deals an additional 2d6 (7) dmg. Target becomes glowing on-hit. Arrow can be removed as a standard action.
Windpipes of Whimsy (Su) concentration - Satyrs regularly go out of their way to deface the temples of other gods and receive favor from Frogg Saran by doing so. They can play their pan flute as a swift action gaining the benefits of the Oh my Frogg technique.

The satyr is talented enough to play without use of its hands.

Weaknesses/Deep Lore

Horny - When presented with the opportunity to get physical with attractive young people, satyr are just about willing to give up any previous allegiances. Such creatures need only offer their bodies to do so.
