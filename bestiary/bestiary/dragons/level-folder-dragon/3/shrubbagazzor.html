Shrubbagazzor

The shrubbagazzor is a creature born of magical manipulation of topiary. These creatures can also be born in response to the mistreatment of the natural world to fight against corruption.

Level 3 Large Dragon

Defense Traits

AC 19
HP 44
Poise 28 DRA 2
Saves Fort +8, Refl +3, Will +10 adv vs fear

Steady Flight - Dragons cap at clumsy 2 while flying.
Stomper - Dragons do not provoke AoOs from leaving a threatened square against creatures that are knocked down.
Take Root healing recharge- At the start of its turn as a swift action while ground borne, a shrubbagazzor can cause roots to shoot out of its legs and burrow into the ground, allowing it to take the total defense action and reduce its speed to 0 this turn.

If it remains rooted (aka it doesn't move or isn't forcefully moved). It also regains 10 +HP at the start of its next turn. This ability can only be used once a minute.
Resistance - Poison

Offense Traits (DC 15)

Bite (P) +6 +4 MV, +4 Dirty Trick
2d8+3 (12) +Jitters
Claw (S) +6 +4 MV
2d8+3 (12)

Multiattack - Make a claw and bite attack

Innate Techniques - Nourish, Tree Shape, Pack Attack
1/day - Wall of Sheep, Fetch, Ants in your Pants, Power of the Sun, Inheritor's Smite
Constant - Courage

New Life Companion - If a shrubbagazzor casts a 🍃Mother Earth spell on a space where there is vegetation growing, it also causes a whelp(agazzor) to grow from it, unless it has already animated 2 of them. Compared to a normal whelp, it has the plant creature type, poison resistance and the Shrubbagazing ability, but no breath weapon.
Breath Weapon Recharge - Against plant creatures, the rose breath heals 6d6 (21) hp. Against non 🪴plants it fascinates for 1 round (will negates). Creatures with scent have disadvantage on this save. 40 ft cone
To recharge this effect, a shrubbagazzor must restore hp from its Take Root ability.
Tail Swipe - As a swift action while grounded, a dragon can make a quick turnaround and swipe at foes it threatens with its tail. Creatures take (10+LvL) poise damage (reflex halves). Poise broken creatures are knocked down.
Jitters - The fragrant breath of a shrubbagazzor is a powerful energizer with an equally powerful crash. On hit on a bite attack, a creature gains +10 speed on their next turn, but become fatigued immediately after. A creature can only be affect by this once each day.
Wing Buffet - As an immediate action when taking flight or landing, a dragon can flap its wings rapidly to whip up a whirlwind. Creatures within 20 ft are knocked down (fort negates). A creature can choose to forgo its save, automatically getting knocked down, to make an AoO if the dragon is within reach.

Weaknesses/Deep Lore

Stop and Smell the Flowers - While in tree shape, a shrubbagazor can have flowery vines growing along its trunk and branches. Creatures with scent always get a perception check to notice something odd about the tree.

If they fail the perception check, they are affected as per the Jitters ability.

Other Traits

Ability Scores - STR +3, DEX +0, LUK +0, INT +0, WIS +3, CHA +2
Languages - Draconic, common, sylvan, druidic
Senses - Darkvision, Echolocation (30 ft), Scent (10 ft)
Shrubbagazing - A shrubbagazor can see through greenery like shrubs, leaves and whatnot as though they were transparent. It also gives this ability to whelps it summons.
Dragon Flight - Dragons can take flight without using a swift action, but they must land by the end of their turn if they do so.
When allowed to fly long distances high in the sky, a dragon can fly 600 miles a day, or 1000 if it fatigues itself. It can continue moving this fast for consecutive days by becoming drained 5 each day it does.
Deific Boons - Spirit Animal, The Forest has Ears, Nature's Vigilance
Speed - 30 Land Speed, 30 Fly
Feats - Perfect Positioning, Divine Interference, Maneuver Versatility, Maneuver Master
Skills - +8 (+4 scent) 👀+Perception, +10 🤔+Insight, +7 🍃+Nature, +8 🏊+Athletics
