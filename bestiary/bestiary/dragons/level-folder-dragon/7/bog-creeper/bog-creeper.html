Bog Creeper

Crust

A pestilent creature with creatures jittering around just underneath its scales, this dragon has bad intentions and worse breath. These creatures prefer the semi aquatic slurry of swamps to the air.

Level 7 Large Dragon/Undead Boss

Defense Traits

AC 23 +2 Bone Cage
HP 130
Poise 30
Saves Fort +12, Refl +8, Will +7

Steady Flight - Dragons cap at clumsy 2 while flying.
Stomper - Dragons do not provoke AoOs from leaving a threatened square against creatures that are knocked down.
Immune - Poison, Disease
Resistance - Necrotic
Reformation - Instead of being defeated, a bog creeper immediately dissipates into several bone beetles that all appear 30 ft away from where it's defeated. These beetles act on the bog creepers turn. If any remain at the end of the bog creepers next turn (including any it summoned previously), the bog creeper reforms at the point closest to all that remain with (# of beetles x10) hp.
Carrion Consumption Healing recharge - Bog creepers often keep decaying and diseased creatures inside of its lair for later consumption. As a swift action, a bog creeper can consume such a creature to gain 20 temp hp and recharge its breath weapon.

Offense Traits (DC 20)

Bite (P) +10
2d8+3 (12)
Claw (S) +10
2d8+3 (12)

Multiattack - Make a tail and bite attack, but not against the same creature

Innate Techniques - Blight (U4), Bone Cage
1/day -

Breath Weapon Recharge poison - Unleash a wave of putrid and caustic liquid. 60 ft cone, 7d6 (24) poison damage and applying sickened 1 (reflex halves dmg and negates sickened). In its wake, it leaves behind hazardous waste that deals 2d6 (7) poison damage to any creatures that end their turn in the area.

Recharges after 1 minute or after using reformation/carrion consumption.
Bad Breath poison - For 1 round after using its breath weapon, or carrion consumption, a bog creeper emits a horrific stench. Creatures it bites during this time become sickened 2. Any creature within 10 ft of the bog creeper during this time is made sickened 1 (fort negates). Creatures with the scent ability have disadvantage on this save.

Tail Swipe - As a swift action while grounded, a dragon can make a quick turnaround and swipe at foes it threatens with its tail. Creatures take (10+LvL) poise damage (reflex halves). Poise broken creatures are knocked down.
Wing Buffet - As an immediate action when taking flight or landing, a dragon can flap its wings rapidly to whip up a whirlwind. Creatures within 20 ft are knocked down (fort negates). A creature can choose to forgo its save, automatically getting knocked down, to make an AoO if the dragon is within reach.

Boss Actions - Usable 1/turn
Back and Better than Ever - After using Reformation, use breath weapon.
Freedom! companion - After taking damage, summon a Bone Beetle. All beetles are companions.
