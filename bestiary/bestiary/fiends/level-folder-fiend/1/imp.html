Imp

This ornery fiend is quick to change its mood, always looking for guidance from one of its superiors. Once they give the signal, they ready their claws and light themselves ablaze.

Level 1 Small Fiend (minion)

Defense Traits

AC 16
HP 30
Poise 21
Saves Fort +1, Refl +5, Will +1 +4 Toxin, venom

Resistance - Fire, Poison
Tactical Retreat - An imp can 🏳Disengage as a swift action.

Offense Traits (DC 13+2)

Claw (S) +3 +2 while ablaze
2d6+2 (9)

Multiattack - Make a shove attack against an ally with a +20 bonus. Make a claw attack or cast Blazing Palm (if ablaze).

Blaze It fire - While ablaze, an imp gets a +2 bonus to attack rolls and the DCs of its offense traits. They can also innately cast Blazing Palm. They can ignite themselves or douse the fire on themselves as a swift action.
Ride and/or Die - An imp can 🐴mount a willing ally as a swift action. While mounted, the ally also automatically "benefits" from its Blaze It feature and gets a +10 bonus to its speed. An imp can also mount an enemy by poise breaking them. If the imp is ablaze, so to becomes its new mount.
¿Quires? poison recharge - While under the effects of Blaze It, an imp can expel a large quantity of toxic gas at a creature adjacent to it, dealing 3d6 (10) poison damage and applying sickened 1 (fort halves damage and negates sicken).
Camouflage

Weaknesses/Deep Lore

Weakness - Holy 3
Cowardly - When it falls below 10 hp, an imp runs for its life, begs for mercy or attempts to otherwise evade death at all costs. With a successful intimidation check, you can get an imp as a companion until this ability procs again.

Certain circumstances can prevent this ability from proccing.
