Convergence (fire) (necrotic) Su - As a swift action, the chained oblivion can set in motion a process that will all but guarantee the destruction of their enemies. The chained oblivion chooses 2 unoccupied points within 60 ft on the ground within 60 ft of each other. In one point a fiery orb appears. In the other, one covered in bone.

At the start of each the chained oblivion's turn, the orbs move 10 ft closer to each other, dealing 5d6 (17) fire or necrotic damage (depending on which orb) to any creature they bump into. When the orbs are adjacent to each other, they fuse and begin to fly around the battlefield.

Each turn the orbs move in a line 40 feet, dealing 5d6 (17) fire damage and 5d6 (17) necrotic damage (reflex halves) to any creature they pass through. This effect persists until the chained oblivion is defeated.

With an 🏊+Athletics check, a Dispel effect or any other reasonable effect to prevent the movement of these orbs, their convergence can be halted for one round. This delaying effect happens instead of being completely removed by dispel or completely destroyed through athletics.

When the chained oblivion is defeated, the orbs disappear.

Weaknesses/Deep Lore

Infernal Seal - The Chained Oblivion are a set of fiends that were too powerful to control and too numerous to rule without endless fighting between factions. As such, the 👿Demon Lords decided to seal them away for later use. Powerful creatures that serve the interests of demon lords can summon these creatures in their dormant state, where they appear in some type of magical seal. This sealed form varies from case to case, but is usually large and unwieldy, weighing several tons and is big enough to contained the chained oblivion.

This seal can be broken away with a specific password (which can gleaned through a DC 40 +Planes check), the destruction of a particular object used as part of the seal, or a DC 40 Brute Force check.

Even when the seal is broken, it still takes 2 full rounds (about 12 seconds) for the creature to awaken, during which it is in Stasis. At the end of the second round, they unleash a devastating burst, activating their Awaken ability and ending their turn.

With a +Planes check, you can slow or halt this process as a standard action while adjacent to the seal. A result of 30 or higher slows the process by 1 round and a result of 40 stops it completely and prevents its reactivation for 1 minute.
Defeat - The chained oblivion cannot die from being Defeated. While defeated, they are Invincible. After 1 minute of being defeated, they return back to being in stasis, as per the Infernal Seal feature. During this minute, creatures can determine what the methods are for unsealing the creature again.
