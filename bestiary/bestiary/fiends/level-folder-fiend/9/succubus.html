Succubus

A feminine fiend with curves in all the right places snaps her whip-like tail at friend and foe alike, all thankful for such a thoughtful blessing.

Level 9 Medium Fiend (Mastermind)

Defense Traits

AC 23
HP 74
Poise 27
Saves Fort +9, Refl +12, Will +13 +4 Charm

Sweet Essence Healing - Whenever a succubus inflicts drain or Horny, they heal 5 hp.

Offense Traits (DC 21)

Tail Whip (S) +12 (+10 reach during turn, +8 grapple, +2 drag, +2 topple)
d6+3 (6) +1 stack of Horny
Stomp (C) +12 (only usable against Knocked Down targets)
2d8+3 (12) +1 stack of Horny

Multiattack - Make 2 Whip attacks. One against an ally and one against an enemy.

Innate Techniques (CL 10) - Unnatural Lust, Detect Thots, Detect Thoughts, Charming Gaze, Fake Out, Command, Teleblock
1/day - Domination, Feign Defeat, Order 66, The Hand that Feeds (U5)
Constant - Sympathy, Sense Motives

Horny Jail - On-hit for its attacks, whenever a creature fails a save against one of its spells or when a creature is Charmed by a succubus, it gains a stack of Horny. Horny stacks intensify physical sensation, functioning as Weakness equal to horny value. The succubus' DC increases by a value equal to the horny stacks on a particular creature. Upon becoming horny 3, a creature becomes Staggered for 1 round. Upon becoming horny 6, the creature gets put in Time Out.
These stacks disappear once every succubus is defeated.
Constrict - Drain 5
Kiss Su - As part of maintaining a Grapple, or as a melee attack, a succubus can kiss an enemy to make them Charmed for 1 round and add one stack of Horny.
Sudden Betrayal- The succubus can use +Disguise in place of +Stealth to Surprise, or if the combat has already started, to inflict DD on the turn of the reveal. Only functions once per combat, and only if the succubus started the combat while in a Polymorph effect.
Whipped Into Shape - A succubus' tail functions as a whip. A succubus can use this whip against allies that are a lower LvL than it. Instead of the usual effects, it allows them to count as the succubus' Companion and grant the creature their teamwork feats. This companionship lasts until they whip another creature while they already have 2 companions. They can AoO allies that move within their natural reach.

Weaknesses/Deep Lore

Rejection Vulnerability - Whenever a succubus fails a CHA Ability Check or Skill Check, they take 1 psychic damage. A creature can automatically do 1+CHA damage to the succubus by insulting their appearance, taste in hats etc (standard).
Weakness - 3 holy

Languages - Telepathy, Infernal, Common, Celestial
Polymorph (Su) poly, true - A succubus can match the appearance of any playable 🏁race. Transforming into a creature is a standard action, but returning to its true form is a free action.
Senses - Nightvision
Tail - The succubus has a prehensile tail that can finely manipulate objects up to 15 ft away.
Profane Gift (Su) Scrying - As part of an intimate encounter. You can confer a gift of +1 to an ability score (max +3) on up to 3 willing creatures. Gifted creatures also receive other effects.

  • You can telepathically communicate with these creatures over any distance.
  • They are permanently charmed by you, which cannot be removed without first removing the gift.
  • You can scry these creatures.
  • You form a 📜summoning contract with these creatures. You can summon one as a standard action. This portion of the ability has the companion tag.
