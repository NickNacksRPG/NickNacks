+Magic Weapon Properties

These properties can be attached to most weapons. Classes like ⚔Fighter and Magus can add these properties to their weapon temporarily.

A weapon can typically only be enchanted with one of these properties. Weapons with additional enchantment slots can get more than 1.
Such enchantments cost 5,000 gold.

Name Effect
Bane +2 att/dmg vs a specific creature type (🪴Plants, Giants, etc).
Your AC is 2 lower when you bane a creature type you possess.
Benevolent Increase the bonus of the aid action by +2
- Only usable while in combat
- Only usable while wielding this weapon
- Doesn't stack with itself (ie multiple benevolent aids only give +4 on the first aid, then +2 for each one after)
Diving This weapon doesn't have reduced effectiveness during Aquatic Combat.
Elemental Weapon can swap between its usual damage type(s) and a specific type of elemental damage (determined on creation) as a free action 1/turn.
Fortuitous Once per round, when the wielder of a fortuitous weapon hits with a simple weapon attack as an AoO, they can make a second simple weapon attack of opportunity with this weapon against that foe at a –4 penalty.

Doesn't consume one of your bonus AoOs from something like Combat Reflexes.
Ghost Touch Weapon treats incorporeal creatures as corporeal and disables their ability to move through solid objects On-hit for 1 round.
Grounding Attacks inflict grounded for 1 round on-hit (fort negates)
Invigorating When you defeat a creature with this weapon, you lose the Fatigued condition. If you are not fatigued, get +10 speed this turn.
Locking On-hit you can lock the weapon in place, entangling the creature struck (reflex negates). While entangling a creature, you cannot use the weapon.
Mimicing Attacks made with this weapon On-hit against a creature allows the wielder to copy one of the creatures elemental resistance types for one round. You can only have one copied resistance at a time. On hit the wielder has an intuitive sense the creature is resistant to, allowing them to choose.

The resistance wears off after 1 minute.
Anchored On-hit apply anchored for 1 round (will negates).
Radiant The wielder can activate the weapon to use Radiance as an Innate Technique once per day, anchored to the weapon.
Returning As a free action, you can call your weapon back to your hand. If it is within 60 feet of you it returns instantly. Anything beyond requires 3 rounds. If there are any physical barriers preventing its return, this does not work.
If a creature possesses the weapon (reflex negates).
Seeking Weapon attacks ignore Partial Concealment, Total Concealment and other forms of miss chance created by the environment or magic.
Sticky DR 10 vs ALL disarm, not just against the enchanted item
Transformative As a swift action you can change your weapon into another type of weapon listed on the Weapon Examples page.
Vicious +2 dmg. On-hit the wielder takes 2 True Damage.
