Pack Mule

Crust

Pack mule is designed to give you the authentic DnD martial experience where you experience just how much of a "team game" it is by being a useless blob of defensive stats. You are almost completely reliant on your team to defeat your enemies. You scale incredibly poorly offensively, but you scale incredibly well defensively.

Note:
This class was intentionally designed obtusely in order to try and recreate the feeling of playing an unoptimized martial character in 5e/PF1e. Nick Nacks was intentionally designed from the ground up to make this kind of thing hard to happen by accident. So many techniques have baseline effectiveness that I had to use very heavy-handed methods to achieve the desired result.

You will need to find out creative ways to be useful outside of what you're used to. Good Luck!

+HP 20(+20 per level)
Poise 20
Skills 4
+Saving Throw Fort, Refl, Will
Memory 4+(2*LvL)

1 - Techniques

You have Martial Specialization
You gain a technique point at level 1, and another every 2 levels after.
These points can be used on the 🛡Protector tree.

1 - Weenie

This class fundamentally alters how many aspects of your character scales offensively.

  • You only gain 1/2 LvL to CL instead of the full LvL value (minimum 0).
  • Your DC is 7+LvL instead of 12+LvL (even when using items made my others)
  • Whenever you deal +HP or Poise damage, that value is cut in half. This uniquely also affects True Damage.
    These effects are also applied to companions.

1 - Battered and Bruised

This class also fundamentally how many aspects of your character scales defensively.

  • You have Regeneration equal to your LvL
  • You gain Resistance to being Drained (cutting any drain inflicted upon you in half)
  • You get a +10 bonus to luck checks to determine the type of affliction you acquire when you are defeated.
  • You have Recovery

1 - Maximum Lift

  • Your Carry Weight is multiplied by itself. Calculate it as normal and then do (carry weight)^2
  • You get 10 additional 🎒inventory slots.
  • 🏊+Athletics proficiency with a +4 bonus.
  • +4 bonus to all STR-based ability checks

1 - Team Game

  • You can take the Carry action as a swift action. When you carry a defeated creature, they are protected from all harmful effects.
  • You can take the Aid action as a swift action.
  • You gain a teamwork feat.

2 - Talents

Gain one at this level and levels 4, 6, 8 and 10. Numbers next to abilities are level requirements.

2 - Secret Compartment

You get +Sleight of Hand as a bonus skill with a +4 bonus.
You can choose a single item no larger that 1 inventory slot to get an additional +8 bonus. It requires a standard action to retrieve something from your Secret Compartment.

2 - Extra Pockets

  • +1,000 carry weight
  • +10 inventory slots
  • +2 to STR-based ability checks
    You can select this talent multiple times.

2 - Planning Ahead

Gain the Brilliant Planner feat.
The amount of gold you can invest in it is (1,000*LvL)

2 - Action Surge recharge

As a free action, gain a standard action.
Usable 1/minute.

2 - Versatility

Gain a ☯+Multiclass feat and the Signature Skill feat.

2 - Esoteric Knowledge (recharge)

Once per game session you can roll any Skill Check with a bonus of 2xLvL.
