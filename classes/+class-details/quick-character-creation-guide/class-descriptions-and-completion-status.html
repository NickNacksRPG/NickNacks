Class Descriptions and Completion Status

This section primarily exists to give you a starting point to see what you're interested in.

Because this is early access, not all the classes are done

  • (C) denotes that the class is complete, often with a variety of options.
  • (P) denotes the class is playable, but lacking in variety or later levels are sparse
  • (ND) denotes the class is still in not done, and is largely unplayable
Name/Status Description
Alchemist (P) The scientist class. One of the more versatile classes that allows you to specialize in different types of fighting. Specialize in poisons, bombs, physical augmentations and more!
Every single alchemist is completely unique thanks to their scientific discoveries.
Beast Trainer (P) The class name says it all: Form a close bond with a special animal uniquely trained to fight by your side. Beast companions can be tailored to fit very specific niches, or you can make a really big dino that chomps people. Also comes with a side of nature magic.
🩸Blood Hunter (P) It's definitely not Bloodborne. A gunman with a crimson tint. You can sacrifice your life to continuously fire your firearm for massive damage. You fight to the last breath and hunt those that escape the bite of your bullets.
💢Barbarian (C) The true bruiser class. The barbarian is designed to run straight into the middle of the battlefield all alone as their optimal strategy. They specialize in taking and dealing damage and equal measure, as well as disrupting the battlefield with combat maneuvers and raw strength, often in ridiculous ways.
🎸Bard (C) The cheerleader class. That's a bit of an exaggeration, but the bard is primarily concerned with supporting their team by inspiring them with tunes. Bards are at home in the middle of their team where they can provide the most benefit. They often excel in social situations. Their performances assure that whatever hijinks your party gets up to goes down in history.
Bender (C) The elemental class. Based largely off of the "Avatar: The Last Airbender" and "Legend of Korra" anime. Based off of what element you choose, you will get a variety of abilities tailored to manipulating that element. 🌪Airbenders are mobile with the ability to glide and eventually fly. 🔥Firebenders specialize in dealing damage. 🪨Earthbenders are tough to keep down and bend the element with the most utility. 🌊Waterbenders are the best generalists with decent durability, healing and CC.
🌍Druid (C) The nature mage class. Focus on a particular element of nature, like beasts, plants, insects or fungi. This choice informs the nature of many of your class features. The druid excels in off-road adventures to the uninhabited corners of the world.
😈Fiend Shifter (C) The demon class. Tailor yourself towards one specific type of fiend as they are depicted in folklore and popular media. Destroyers do just that and grow to monstrous sizes with extra demon parts to get the job done. 💋Succ shifters primarily deal in deals, using the power of persuasion to lock people into promises as well as lock enemies in their embrace. There are more, but I'll leave it at that for brevity.
⚔Fighter (C) Do they even really need a description? Your standard warrior class whose primary asset is their mastery over their equipment. Archetypes give variety in your game plan whether its zone control with Dark Knight, dueling with 🤺Swashbuckler or horde slaying with Aran.
🪵Classes/Hero/Hero (C) A class based off of the silliness encountered in the Oldschool Runescape MMORPG. It is very flexible, with an emphasis on adding tons of new abilities that interact with skills, similar to the Signature Skill feat.
Hunter (ND) A simple martial class meant to emulate the vague ideas surrounding whatever a "Ranger" is in DnD. They use ranged weapons, excel at tracking and...other things I'm sure eventually.
Healer (C) The true support class. Provides the invaluable resource of healing, whether it means +HP, poisons or whatever other strange ailments that befall adventures.
🤡Jester (C) Bard felt too general for me, so they're primarily musicians now. Jesters are kind of an in between for bard and rogue. Part martial, part mage, with tons of supernatural abilities that allow them to get into and out of trouble based being an Actor, Mime, or 🤣Prankster.
🐸Lunatic (C) The wild magic class. A full blown magic caster class similar to 5e's wild magic sorcerer. This class is meant to be a complete joke as far as flavor text goes. If you want your character to be completely unpredictable and completely insane, this is the one for you. Praise 🐸Frogg Saran!
🌞Light Guardian (C) Light guardians are duty-bound to protect the world from the seemingly endless onslaught of fiends and mages who would take advantage of others. A very simple martial support class centered around preventing and protecting against magic and 👿Fiends.
👊Martial Artist (C) The unarmed class. Your primary asset is your body and your spirit. Martial artists require no equipment in order to be functional, replacing weapons and armor with years of rigorous training. This class covers everything from Wrestlers to Monks to 🍺Barroom Brawlers.
Magus (ND) The spell sword class. Not quite fighter not, quite wizard, the magus specializes in dealing damage with magic via their weapons. They get unique abilities that allow them to combine spells and attacks.
💎Mana Addict (P) Armor of Agathys, the class. When built around, the 5e spell is completely bonkers. I felt it necessary to give it its own class. Outside of that, they have unique ways to manipulate and steal mana that other classes down. Rare anti-mage technology.
Maligner (C) A class with a very specific theme. You no longer exist. Instead you are a set of interchangeable and replaceable personas. There is no "real you." Most of the maligner's abilities are centered around deceit through illusions, enchantments and transformations.
Mechanic 1 (ND) The mecha class. Has e variants: Siege Engineer, Classes/Mechanic/Pilot and Suitor. Siege engineers are the literal tanks of Nick Nacks. Pilots are the mecha classic where you hop into an oversized robot and use that for fighting, transportation etc. Suitors prefer a technologically advanced body suit (a la Metroid) that enhances their combat prowess and comes with all kinds of useful adaptations and gadgets.
👁Mesmerist (C) The hypnotist class. You have a very special pair of eyes that allow you to break the mental barriers of creatures just by staring at them. You cast spells that manipulate the minds of others and have a special set of psychic implants that you can use to create contingent effects like seeing through another creature's eyes or turning them into a sleeper cell.
💀Necromancer (C) The undead master class. Every enemy that you defeat you are able to transform into an allied skeleton or zombie. Your abilities allow you to better maintain these creatures and also makes them more powerful. 💀Medium uses supernatural powers to interact with the dead. Death Knight gets into the thick of things with their minions and drains the blood of their enemies. 👻Restless Spirit is practically immortal with the ability to return to life after death on a regular basis.
😇Paladin (P) The good boy class. Paladins are shielded by their faith in god as much as their armor. They always take the moral high ground and have mercy for other creatures. Some creatures though, like undead and demons, are beyond redemption, and must be eliminated by using their divine powers to smite them into oblivion.
🐴Pack Mule (ND) What if a class was basically nothing but a pile of meat points that did no damage? This is more of a thought experiment at the moment to test the limits of the game.
Psychic (C) The psionic class. Your psychic powers allow you to manipulate objects with your mind, gaze into the minds of others and even manipulate their memories. Telekineticist specializes in the telekinesis, throwing objects (and creatures) around the battlefield. 🔍Spy that specializes in scrying: A type of advanced magical spying technique that allows you to hear and see things from great distances. Fate Spinner specializes reading and toying with the future.
Rogue The class that isn't afraid to use underhanded tactics. Rogue specialize in stealthy operations, are good with their hands and have high mobility. Rogues can be 🧠Masterminds, creating nebulous plans that fall into place precisely when needed. Rogues can also be common Thugs, specializing in bullying and intimidation.
Shapeshifter (C) The animal transformer class. The shapeshifter has the ability to take on any number of features from different types of creatures, but mostly animals. They can choose to go an anthropomorphic hybrid mode, growing claws and jaws werewolf style, or they can choose to completely transform into animals. The wide variety of features that a shapeshifter can use and the swiftness of the transformations allows them to adapt to the situation on the fly.
Summoner (???) This is mostly just a placeholder. I have plans...
The pokemon trainer class. The summoner summons a unique creature called an eidolon that fights by their side. As the summoner grows more powerful, they are able to shape the course of the creatures evolution, allowing them to create completely unique abominations from other realms. Outside of the eidolon, they're also able to summon other weaker creatures to supplement their main damage source.
🐇Slayer (C) The Doom class. Based largely off of the games "Doom: 2016" and "Doom Eternal." You are a one-man army with a deadly arsenal of unique guns, melee weapons and supernatural powers almost entirely dedicated to killing things. Even without these weapons, your fists are usually enough to get the job done.
Also added an archetype called Parry Fiend based on Dark Ages.
Virtuoso (C) Another unique gun wielding class. Primarily based off of the league of legends character Jhin. The virtuoso has a spectacular flair to their combat, creating flashy displays out of their gunplay. They have supernatural abilities that allow them to murder people in a spectacular fashion. Fear the 4th shot.
Wizard (P) The magic caster with the biggest brain. Wizards possess the unique ability to memorize their spells on the fly. Given enough time for preparation, a skilled wizard can overcome any problem, including creating entirely new spells. A ⏰Chronomancer can go back in time to change events when they don't like the outcome. Financial wizards accumulate power as they accumulate wealth to an extreme degree. Destruction wizards are the most powerful mages around when it comes to defeating enemies.
