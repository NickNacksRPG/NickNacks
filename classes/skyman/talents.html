Talents

Abilities that are preceded by a number have a level requirement.

Combat Talents

Potion Chugger - You can drink any potions as a free action 1/turn.

Staggering Shield Recharge - Your shield attacks stagger On-hit (fort negates). A creature can only be subject to this effect once a minute.
Ker-plink - When you take the Shields Up action against a Ranged Weapon Attack, you (or the target you shield) negate its damage entirely. This only functions for 1 attack with shields up and doesn't work against siege weapons.
Bowl 'em Over - You gain the Trample ability while you wield a shield, dealing 2d6+LvL crush damage.

Paralyzed - As an instant action when you would take Damage or suffer a status effect, you can instead choose to stun yourself until the end of your next turn.
Face Tank Healing - Whenever you provoke an AoO, gain 10 Temp HP.

Just Your Imagination - Gain the Sniper feat. If you successfully snipe an enemy to ambush and the enemy fails their 👀+Perception check, combat does not start.

Buy One, Get One Free - Gain the Bonded Weapon feat. If you bind to a light or one-handed weapon, you get an exact copy of that weapon, including all materials, magic weapon properties, etc. One of them is always considered the original, and if it is taken from you for longer than 1 minute, the copy disappears.

Gift of Giving - You can use Thievery to place a Toxin on an unaware creature's person somewhere (the skyman is very creative. Not even creatures without pockets are safe).  At any point before the creature realizes it’s been planted and disposes of it, the Skyman may activate the poison as a free action, as though fulfilling whatever the usual delivery method is.

(10) Bucket Su - You can 🐴Mount a common bucket, giving you a Fly speed equal to your land speed. If you are ever Poise broken, or otherwise separated from your bucket, you plummet.

(4) Horse Technology (Su) Teleportation - Gain the Pet feat, and the creature must be a horse. With a whistle (free action), you can teleport this horse by your side. If this horse dies, it is banished to the horse dimension and you cannot summon it again until you rest.
Your horse also shares your Blessing of the Mountain Goat feature.

(4) Combat Caster Sp - Choose an elemental Damage tag. You permanently memorize level 1 spell with that elemental damage tag.
Gain a Multiattack:
Cast a level 1 spell with your chosen elemental damage tag and make a Melee Attack.
Combat Medic Sp - You permanently memorize level 1 spell with the Healing tag.
Gain a Multiattack:
Cast a level 1 spell with the healing tag and make a Melee Attack.
Martial Prowess - Gain a Multiattack:
Make 2 attacks.

(4) Housecarl Companion - Gain the assistance of a basic companion. They are a human ⚔fighter with no archetype, no alternate racial features and only combat feats.

  • Their Level is your level-3
  • They have starting stats of STR +3, DEX +1, LUK +0, INT +0, WIS +0, CHA +0
    If your housecarl dies, you must pay either their family or their employer 5,000g before you can acquire a new one.

Utility Talents

Courier - You have a loyal squad of three couriers that always appear at your beck and call. They can carry up to 10 pounds and will always reach their destination within one day. These couriers know the safest routes and generally refuse to go to particularly dangerous locations. If killed, get a letter from a replacement telling you about the last one’s death within 24 hours and all of your couriers take a 2 week vacation.

Fast Travel (teleportation) (recharge) - 1/week you can teleport to any Teleportation Circles. This ritual costs 200g of a rare white chalk and 1 minute to perform. You can bring up to 8 other creatures, provided they are no larger than large size.

Roaming Alchemist - You can find alchemy crafting materials in all manner of places. While travelling out and about in the world, you can generate materials as though using your Alchemy skill as a profession, but it is only half as effective.

Master Enchanter - You gain an additional skill proficiency that must be spent on a crafting 🧑‍🌾+Profession. You gain an additional underwear slot that can function as an additional item slot for any type of item, but it cannot be medium armor or heavy armor.

Just Checking - You can freely check a creature's pockets, bags etc to see what they are carrying on their person without scrutiny. Stealing it still requires Thievery.

I Belong Here - All creatures of your level or lower treat you as though you belong where you are.
You can walk into people's homes uninvited, and for no reason. You can exist within a royal palace like you lived there. NPCs do the mental gymnastics required to justify you existing wherever you are. There are some upper limits when it comes to interacting with NPCs, like sleeping in their beds.
You can do anything you like, so long as it does not break any major laws, like theft, assault etc. Even still, if nobody notices you do these things, who can say it was you?

Ragdoll - You can appear dead as a standard action, rendering yourself Helpless, but still aware of your surroundings. Only a 💉+Medicine check (DC 20+LvL) can determine that you’re faking it.  When you “die,” you can set a condition (I hear the word bazinga) or a duration to awaken.

Dialogue Skip - Gain the +Persuasion skill. You can use it to Gather Information in d4 minutes. You can automatically end a conversation with an NPC with no repercussions, as though the conversation had ended naturally.

Fast Travel?
You can make it to any destination in the world in 3 days flat. The location you decided in cannot be a specific latitude and longitude coordinate and must therefore have some kind of name or significance, such as a settlement or a natural wonder. If the location of this place is widely unknown, and your group is not an exception, you cannot travel to it.
