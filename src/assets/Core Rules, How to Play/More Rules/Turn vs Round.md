A lot of care and attention has been given to every ability in the game with regards to the terminology "round" and "turn." For maximum clarity I write this page to distinguish the two.

A ROUND in combat refers to the list of creatures in initiative. The round ends when the last creature in initiative acts. A new round begins at the start of initiative. This means you technically regain all of your 1/round resources, such as your [[AoO]] and your [[Swift Action]], which you can potentially use to perform an [[Immediate Action]] like [[Block]] against an incoming attack.

A TURN in initiative refers to an individual creature's turn. Many effects will specify turn, such as damage over time effects like [[Bleeding]] and [[Ablaze]].

If you add up all of the TURNS in initiative, you get a ROUND.
