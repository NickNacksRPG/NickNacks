Creature takes a -30 penalty to speed and attacks get a +2 bonus to attack against the creature for 1 round, or longer if [[Held Down]]. Affects [[Fly|flying]] creatures as normal (abstract it as you wish).

Knocked down creatures cannot [[jump]].

A creature can end the knockdown on themselves as a standard action.
