Reduce speed to 0, representing when a creature is knocked into the air and kept in place for an extended duration rather than high into the air. This is a condition you naturally recover from after 1 round unless stated otherwise (similar to [[Knocked Down]]).

A creature can end the airborne condition through taking [[Fly|flight]], becoming [[grounded]], or any team play that can reasonably snatch someone out of the sky. A creature can also attempt to [[breakout]] from being airborne as a swift action, but using [[+Acrobatics]] instead of [[+Athletics]].
