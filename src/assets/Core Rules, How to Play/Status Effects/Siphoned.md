The creature is severed from its magic. It is not capable of casting [[Spell|Spells]], [[Concentration]], or activating its [[Su]] abilities. 

Constant effects like from [[prepared]] techniques, effects from items, or effects that were already cast (like [[frost armor]]) remain in effect.

TL;DR - Creature cannot activate abilities that are [[Sp]] or [[Su]]. They cease concentration.
<span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">For example</span>...All [[Beastmode]] techniques are [[Su]]. You still benefit from [[Shells Up]]'s flanking immunity, but you cant spend the mana to pop in the shell and total defense.
