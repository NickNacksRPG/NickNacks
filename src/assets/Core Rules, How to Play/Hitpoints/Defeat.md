When you take damage that reduces you to zero hp, you are [[defeated]], and must be healed to regain consciousness in a timely manner. Most unimportant creatures are assumed to die upon being defeated.

You can choose to non-lethally defeat someone, even retroactively after the battle, causing them to survive the encounter (if they don't receive further damage). If this is not the case, creatures get a stack of [[Doom]] on each of their turns, and whenever they take more damage. Every stack after the 3rd they accumulate demands a DC 8 [[luck check]] or they <span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">die</span>. 

<span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">When an enemy healer is present</span>, or other healing options like potions are present, players must be more considerate of how they approach the situation. The GM should consider what the players usually prefer when fighting, mercy or death, or should directly ask players if they want to shoot to kill to avoid situations where the players claim "actually I killed them" once a healer arrives on the scene.

<span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">When a character recovers from the defeated condition</span>, they are [[dazed]] on their next turn.

- When recovering from the defeated condition, you also gain an [[Affliction]], gained after any combat to save time.
- When a [[Main Character Status|main character]] (ie usually a player) falls to zero HP, they are never at risk of instant death and do not accumulate doom passively.
- See [[Recovering from Defeat]] for what happens when defeated creatures are left to their own devices.
- Every 5 doom stacks you survive, you accumulate a [[Battle Scar|Battle Scar]]
