Dispel refers to when a magical effect is negated, usually functioning as [[Dispel Magic]]. Other effects, such as techniques in the [[Arc]] tree or [[Unique Special Attacks|magic crash]] can also perform a similar function.

The type of bonus is usually specified in the ability. The DC to dispel the ability is typically 10+[[Caster Level|CL]] or 10+[[LvL]] for [[Su]] abilities.
