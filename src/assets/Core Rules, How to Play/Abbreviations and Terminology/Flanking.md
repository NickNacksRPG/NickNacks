Flanking is a [[Circumstance]] that grants +2 [[Attack]] against a target that you and an ally are [[Threaten|Threatening]] from opposite sides. I am generally pretty lenient with granting flanking, especially against larger creatures that can sometimes make the dynamic a bit less straightforward.

If you are not [[Threaten|threatening]], such as if you are [[dazed]]/[[Disarm|disarmed]] or the target has [[Total Concealment]], then you cannot flank.
