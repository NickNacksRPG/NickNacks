A Targeted ability refers to one where you must select targets to apply the effect. Some abilities are particular about what they work on, and will fail if used outside of their scope. You must have [[Line of Effect]] and be able to see your target to target them. 

[[Attack Roll]]s are special in that if you cannot see a creature ([[Total Concealment]]), you must target their space with the intention of targeting them.
