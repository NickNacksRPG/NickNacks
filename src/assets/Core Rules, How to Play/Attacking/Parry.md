Parry is an action granted by class abilities (like [[Swashbuckler]]) and equipment (like [[buckler]] and [[parrying dagger]]). The action type required varies depending on its source.

[[Prepare]] yourself for an incoming attack. When you are attacked, you can choose whether or not to parry it. If you do, roll an attack as an instant action. If your attack roll is higher, the attack is negated and the enemy takes [[Poise]] damage equal to your attack roll.
You can decide to parry an attack after the result of the roll is revealed.

You cannot parry [[+Siege Weapon|siege weapons]].
