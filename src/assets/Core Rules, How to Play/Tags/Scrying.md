Scrying effects create a specific type of magic sensor, often from remote locations, usually for the purpose of spying on people. The sensor allows you to see and hear anything with any of your senses, so long as it is in the specified range the effect mentions.

While scrying, your body is [[Stunned]]. You can return back to your body as a standard action. You are aware of any physical sensation your body experiences while scrying.

Scrying sensors are small, but visible to the naked eye. They usually take on the appearance of a floating eye. Sensors always have [[partial concealment]] as they blend in to the environment. You can use [[Trailing (P)|trailing]] to remain unseen for extended durations. Use your [[DC]] if you are untrained in stealth.

Scrying is a [[Concentration]] effect.
