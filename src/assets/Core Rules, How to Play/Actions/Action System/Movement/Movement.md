You can move up to your [[Land Speed]] on your turn. The default land speed is 30. Some abilities or effects may require spending a certain amount of your movement for the turn (like [[Tank Up]] ). If you have other speeds, you can use those instead (provided you have the water to [[swim]], for instance).

Movement can be used freely between any of your actions/attacks. This means you can move, attack, kill a demon, then move onto the next demon and continue attacking. Some actions and attacks restrict your ability to move (like [[Base Special Attacks|cleave]] and [[Heavy Hitter]]).

Abilities that move you do not cost or consume any of your movement unless otherwise stated.
