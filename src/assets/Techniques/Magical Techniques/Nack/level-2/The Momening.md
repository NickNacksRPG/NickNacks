Level 2 [[Nack]]
Tags: [[Mind-Affecting]] [[Figment]]
Action: Standard

| Range          | 30 ft      |
| -------------- | ---------- |
| Target/[[AoE]] | 1 creature |
| Duration       | 10 minutes |
| Save           | Will       |
Summon an illusion of the target creature's mom, who then gives them a menial task (chores, self hygiene, clean your room etc) that they must then perform (will negates).

Does not function in combat. If the target passes its save, mom disappears.
