Their skin is turned <span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 240)">blue</span> and objects on their person have a <span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 240)">blue</span> tinge to them. This <span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 240)">blue</span>ness is automatically removed by any [[dispel]] effect.
