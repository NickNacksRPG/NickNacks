Level 2 [[Pyromancy]]
Tags: [[Fire]]
Action: Standard

| Range          | -       |
| -------------- | ------- |
| Target/[[AoE]] | Self    |
| Duration       | 1 round |
| Save           | -       |
Surround yourself in a barrier of molten lava, taking the [[Total Defense]] action. The next enemy that deals damage to you triggers a recoil effect that shoots lava at them. They take 2d8+CL (9+CL) fire damage. This does not end the effects of the total defense action.
