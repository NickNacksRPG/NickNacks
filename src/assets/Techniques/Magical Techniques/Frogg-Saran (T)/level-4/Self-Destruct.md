Level 4 [[Frogg Saran (T)]]
Tags: [[Force]], [[Recharge]]
Action: Standard

| Range          | -                                      |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Target/[[AoE]] | 20 ft radius burst, centered on caster |
| Duration       | -                                      |
| Save           | Reflex                                 |
You explode violently, setting yourself to 0 hp and instantly become [[defeated]]. All creatures within 20 ft of you take 20+(2xCL) force damage and are knocked back 20 feet (reflex halves).

Caster gains a T2 [[affliction]].

<span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Note - </span>
- If you have some type of ability that allows you to survive being defeated, like [[Diehard]], then you can use it to prevent being defeated from self-destruct.
- Self destruct specifically doesn't deal damage to yourself to avoid weird interactions with things like [[Unchained]] Fiend Shifter
