Level 1 [[Goomshroom]]
Tags: -
Action: Swift

| Range          | 30 ft      |
| -------------- | ---------- |
| Target/[[AoE]] | 1 creature |
| Duration       | 1 minute   |
| Save           | -          |
Your throat swells as your mouth fills with bile and you spit a glob of goo at a creature. Make a [[Ranged Attack|ranged]] [[spell attack]]. [[On-hit]] their speed is reduced by 15. A creature can remove this goo automatically as a standard action, or they can [[Breakout]] with athletics.
