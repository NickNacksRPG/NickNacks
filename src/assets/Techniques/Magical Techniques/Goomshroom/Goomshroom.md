The [[Shroomlette]] is an entity that represents the bond between goo dragons (gooms), and [[shroomkin]], a race of people with close ties to fey and mushrooms. These two groups live in tandem with each other with one main goal: increase the quantity of happiness in the world.  The tactics they use to do so is to subdue creatures that decrease happiness and physically change the world around them to make it a better place to live.

Shroomlette spells are a mixed bag, but mostly for support and utility. Includes many oddities mimicking the special abilities of gooms and shrooms.
