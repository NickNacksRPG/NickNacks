You can walk on any rainbows as though they were solid surfaces. 

Half of your movement every round can generate solid rainbows that anybody can walk on underneath your feet. The formation of this rainbow follows an arch-like pattern that allows you to ascend at a roughly 45 degree angle, making every 2 spaces forward, 1 space directly up. Rainbows disappear after 1 minute.
