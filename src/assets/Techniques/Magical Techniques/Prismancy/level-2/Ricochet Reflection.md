Level 2 [[Prismancy]]
Tags: [[Light]], [[Concentration]]
Action: Swift

| Range          | 60 ft |
| -------------- | ----- |
| Target/[[AoE]] | -     |
| Duration       | -     |
| Save           | -     |
You create a giant mirror that twists light and projectiles. Any creature that can see the mirror can use it to curve their [[Line of Effect]] and to determine cover. This functions for [[Ranged Attack|ranged attacks]] and line attacks (like [[Wave of Darkness]]), as the mirror curves their trajectories towards whatever you see in the mirror.

When you cast the spell, you can adjust the angle to your liking to target a specific creature you could not see otherwise. Once an angle is decided, you cannot adjust the angle unless you spend another swift action.
