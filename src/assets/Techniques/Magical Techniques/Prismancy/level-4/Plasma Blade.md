Level 4 [[Prismancy]]
Tags: [[Buff]], [[Fire]], [[Light]]
Action: Swift

| Range          | -                                 |
| -------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Target/[[AoE]] | Held melee slash or pierce weapon |
| Duration       | 1 minute                          |
| Save           | -                                 |
Imbue a weapon in hand with searing light, changing its damage to d6+CL (3+CL) fire damage. It becomes weightless. It gives off [[Light Level|bright light]] within 20 feet.

As part of casting this spell, you may make a [[Melee Attack|melee]] [[Simple Attack|simple]] [[Spell Attack]] with this weapon. Every turn after, you can make a simple attack as a swift action. Any attacks you make with it past the first consume an additional 4 mana with each swing.

Apply [[glowing]] for 1 round [[On-hit]] with this weapon.

If the plasma blade leaves your possession, this spell ends after one round. 


<span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Note -</span>
The spirit of this ability is to be used with only a single plasma blade. Additional ways of acquiring plasma blades (including as handoffs from allies) is not intended.
