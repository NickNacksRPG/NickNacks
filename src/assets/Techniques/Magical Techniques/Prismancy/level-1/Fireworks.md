Level 1 [[Prismancy]]
Tags: [[Light]], [[Upcast]], [[Fire]]
Action: Standard

| Range          | 60 ft                                       |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Target/[[AoE]] | 5 ft radius, centered on a point in the air |
| Duration       | -                                           |
| Save           | Reflex                                      |
Fire a tiny glowing ball into the air that explodes on command in a brilliant display of lights. Creatures in the area take (CL) fire damage, become [[blinded]] for 1 round, and [[glowing]] until the end of your next turn (reflex reduces blind to blurred vision and negates fire damage).

If the creature was already glowing, it instead deals (2*[[Caster Level|CL]]) fire damage, the blind and damage effects do not have saving throws and the glowing is ends.

<span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">Upcast </span>- Every 4 [[Caster Level|CL]]s...
Fire an additional exploding projectile. A creature affected by multiple firework explosions receives no additional effect.
