Level 1 [[Prismancy]]
Tags: [[Fire]]
Action: Standard

| Range          | 60 ft       |
| -------------- | ----------- |
| Target/[[AoE]] | 1 creature? |
| Duration       | -           |
| Save           | -           |
Fire a ray of brilliant energy. Make a [[Ranged Attack|ranged]] [[Spell Attack|spell attacks]] that deals 2d8+CL (9+CL) fire damage. If the target is [[glowing]], the light bounces off of them back at you, consuming the glowing and allowing you to make an additional attack against another creature within range. This happens regardless of if you hit or miss.

If you damage the same creature twice with this spell in the same cast, secondary attack(s) do half damage. You can only attack a creature, at most, twice with the same cast of this spell.
