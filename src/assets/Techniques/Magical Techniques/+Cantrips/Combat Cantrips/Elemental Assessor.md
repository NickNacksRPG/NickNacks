You fire a bolt of energy that rapidly changes color and consistency to match varying elements. Make a [[Ranged Attack|Ranged]] [[spell attack]] against a creature that deals d10+CL (5+CL) [[True Damage]]. If the creature possesses a [[Weakness]] to an elemental damage type, this spell instead deals that type of elemental damage, changing its tag as appropriate. When this happens, the color of the spell changes to an identifiable color so the caster knows which damage type the target is vulnerable to.

If a creature has multiple elemental damage weaknesses, this spell does multiple damage types, amplifying its damage with each weakness they possess.
