Level 2 [[Necromancy]]
Tags: [[Psychic]], [[Persistent]], [[Concentration]], [[Upcast]]
Action: Standard

| Range          | 30 ft    |
| -------------- | -------- |
| Target/[[AoE]] | See text |
| Duration       | -        |
| Save           | Will     |
Choose 2 spots within 20 ft of each other on the ground. Two 20 ft tall pillars of spectral energy erupt from the ground and wailing spirits fly between them, effectively creating a translucent barrier. This barrier does not block line of sight or [[Line of Effect]].

Any creature that moves through the pillars, or the line between them, is harassed by these spirits,  taking 3d8+CL (13+CL) psychic damage and becomes [[shaken]] 1 (will halves damage and negates shaken).

<span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">Upcast </span>- Every 4 [[Caster Level|CL]]s...
Increase the distance apart you can place the two pillars by 5 ft.
