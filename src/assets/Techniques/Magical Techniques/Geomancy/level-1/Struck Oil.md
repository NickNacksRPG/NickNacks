Level 1 [[Geomancy]]
Tags: [[Earth]], [[Concentration]]
Action: Standard

| Range          | 30 ft                                           |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Target/[[AoE]] | 10 ft radius, centered on a point on the ground |
| Duration       | 3 rounds                                        |
| Save           | Reflex                                          |
Cause a slippery black liquid to burst from the ground like a fountain. All creatures are [[knocked down]] (reflex negates).

The oil continues to sprinkle the area, causing any creature that steps into the area to be covered in oil. The area is also considered a [[Slippery Surfaces|slippery surface]].

If a creature covered in oil takes [[fire]] damage, they are lit [[ablaze]]. 

If an [[AoE]] effect with the fire tag includes the area of this spell, the spell ends and all creatures in the area are instantly set ablaze. The area is then considered an [[Wild Fire|inferno]]. Other effects can also cause this to happen, such as an ablaze creature moving into the area, or a simple [[torch]].
