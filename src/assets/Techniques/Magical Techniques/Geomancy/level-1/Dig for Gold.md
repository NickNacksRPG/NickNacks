Level 1 [[Geomancy]]
Tags: [[Earth]], [[Concentration]]
Action: Standard

| Range          | 30 ft                                           |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Target/[[AoE]] | 10 ft radius, centered on a point on the ground |
| Duration       | -                                               |
| Save           | Reflex                                          |
The ground explodes as though it was hit with an enormous pickaxe. Creatures take 2d8+CL (9+CL) crush damage and the area becomes [[difficult terrain]]. If the area was already earth-based difficult terrain (stone stairs, quicksand, rubble etc), creatures have [[Advantage|disadvantage]] on their save.

The first time you cast the spell in combat (aka not passively), roll a [[Luck Check]]. If you roll at least a twenty, you find valuable minerals worth (100*[[Caster Level|CL]]) gold.
