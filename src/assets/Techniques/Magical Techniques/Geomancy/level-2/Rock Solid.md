<span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Note</span> - 
This technique can only be used while touching the ground, and more specifically, some form of rock, dirt, sand or earth.

Plant your feet firmly on the ground and bury them. You reduce your speed to 0, but automatically negate the next instance of [[Forced Movement]], being [[Knocked Down]] or being sent [[Airborne]]. 

You can end this technique as a free action.
