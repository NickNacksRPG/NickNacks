Level 6 [[Alchemy (T)]]
Tags: [[Polymorph]]
Action: 1 minute
Focus: A bucket filled with water suspended in midair

| Range          | Reach    |
| -------------- | -------- |
| Target/[[AoE]] | -        |
| Duration       | 24 hours |
| Save           | -        |
You enchant a bucket of water to transform it into a pool of rainbow liquid. You can place any item with a gold value into this bucket while you imagine another item with a gold value or half that items value or less. Over the course of the next minute, the placed item seems to phase through the bottom of the bucket in a new shape before eventually dripping down onto the ground in the desired shape. This transformation is irreversible and permanent.

You cannot place many smaller items to "combine" them into a more expensive item.

In order to shimmer particularly large items, you're going to need a pretty big bucket.
