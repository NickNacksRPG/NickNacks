You are much better at conducting heat in a controlled manner. In general you can exist in hot environments comfortably. Your hands (or equivalent body parts) glow like heated metal and can be used to touch solid objects that are sources of large heat. Up to a 1 ft cube can be affected at a time. By maintaining contact with an object (one [[FRA]]) you can store its heat. Later you can choose to diffuse this heat into the air around you, or transfer it to another object. The effects of which depend on the object. Many things simply burn, but others like stone and metals can be softened.

If you desire, you can immediately diffuse the heat as you touch the hot object continuously. You can affect a 1ft cube per round of contact. Prolonged exposure to heat bothers you less during this spell, but in enclosed spaces, your allies, and eventually even you, can be overwhelmed by heat.

With an empty hand, you can make a [[natural attack]] as a standard action or [[AoO]] that deals 2d8+CL (9+CL) fire damage. This can also be used as a form of [[Constrict]] damage.

If for whatever reason you need your hands to not be superheated, you can submerge them completely in water to cool them off, disabling this technique for the remainder of the day.
