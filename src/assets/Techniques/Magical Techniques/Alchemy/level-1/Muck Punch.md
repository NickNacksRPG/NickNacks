Your forearms and hands become a gooey mess, more closely resembling piles of amorphous sludge. While under the effects of this technique, you can wield other equipment, but cannot make [[Weapon Attack|weapon attacks]], or do anything that requires fine manipulation of the hands (like [[Thievery]]). You can also slip your arms into places that liquids could fit.

With an empty hand, you can make a [[Melee Attack|melee]] [[Spell Attack]] as a standard action or [[AoO]] that deals 2d8+CL (9+CL) poison damage. This can also be used as a form of [[Constrict]] damage.

If for whatever reason you need your hands to not be mucky, you can submerge them completely in water to wash them off, disabling this technique for the remainder of the day.
