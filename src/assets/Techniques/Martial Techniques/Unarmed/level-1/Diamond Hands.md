Your hands are protected from harm, allowing you to avoid the damage from direct contact while grabbing dangerous objects, like grabbing a cactus. This also applies to any creature that harms you by touching you (such as a very hot [[fire giant]]).

Harmful objects touching anything above the wrist are still dangerous as normal.
