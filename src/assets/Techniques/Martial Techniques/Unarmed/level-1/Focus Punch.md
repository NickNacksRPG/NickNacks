Focus your energy into a mighty punch. For 1 round you take a -10 penalty to [[+Stealth]] from you powering up.

If you do not take [[+HP]] damage or get [[poise]] broken by the start of your next turn, you can make an [[Unarmed Attack|unarmed strike]] with +10 [[Attack]] that deals an additional (2*[[LvL]]) dmg at any point during your turn as a free action.

Damage over time effects, such as [[bleeding]], do not end your focus.

You cannot begin charging a focus punch on the same turn you delivered one.
