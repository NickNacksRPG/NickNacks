<span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Note: </span>
Requires initiating from above the foe.

Hop into the air and stomp on a foe so hard they bounce back up, dealing 3d8 (13) bonus damage and inflicting [[Airborne]] [[On-hit]].

If the creature was already airborne, or if they are otherwise in the air, such as by [[Fly|flying]], they are instead on-hit sent them plummeting to the ground (5*[[LvL]]) ft, dealing appropriate [[fall damage]] (ignoring fall damage reducing effects like [[glide]] and [[Cat's Grace]]) and applying [[Clumsy]] 1. Any excess distance not fallen sends them back up into the air again and refreshes airborne.

If you initiate this technique with your own flight, it does not deal the bonus 3d8 damage.
