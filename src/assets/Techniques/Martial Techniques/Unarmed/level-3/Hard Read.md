Level 3 [[Unarmed]]
Tags: [[Elusive]]
Action: Swift

| Range          | 30 ft      |
| -------------- | ---------- |
| Target/[[AoE]] | 1 creature |
| Duration       | -          |
| Save           | -          |
[[Prepare]] to intercept a creature aiming for where they will be instead of where they are, your hands glowing with anticipation. Choose a direction: north, south, east or west. If they move at least 10 ft in that direction, you can activate your prepared action.

Immediately move adjacent to them and roll a [[grapple]] attack with a +20 bonus. Excess [[poise]] damage is halved, then converted into crush damage.

So long as the direction they move includes the direction you chose (ie "north" and the target moves northwest 10 ft), your prepared grab triggers.
