Whenever an ally (other than you) takes [[+HP]] damage or succumbs to a negative [[+Status]] effect as a result of an enemy you [[threaten]], you can make a [[Simple Attack|simple]] [[melee attack]] against that enemy.

This technique follows the same rules as [[Sentinel]] and requires the direct action from an enemy to trigger, and thus does not activate on damage over time effects like [[Bleeding|bleed]], or your ally running into a [[Wall of Fire]].
