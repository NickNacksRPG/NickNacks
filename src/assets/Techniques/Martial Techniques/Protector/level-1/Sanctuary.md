Level 1 [[Protector]]
Tags: [[Su]]
Action: Standard

| Range          | 30 ft      |
| -------------- | ---------- |
| Target/[[AoE]] | 1 creature |
| Duration       | 1 round    |
| Save           | -          |
Affected creature is surrounded by a wall of ethereal shields. It cannot be [[targeted]] by harmful effects such as spells or attacks. The creature can still be affected by things that are [[AoE]], they just can't be directly affected by hostile actions. If the ally is currently being targeted by an effect that requires the active maintenance of another creature, such as a [[grapple]], it immediately ends. 

Creatures can instinctually tell when a creature cannot be targeted by sanctuary, and do not waste their actions to "test" if they can attack the creature. If the target takes aggressive actions, the shields shatter and the effect ends.

A creature who has been affected by this technique cannot have it used on them again for 1 round.
