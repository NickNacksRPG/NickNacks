You radiate bright light within 30 ft and a tether forms between you and any allies in the aura. You take half of their [[+HP]] damage as [[True Damage]] (with odd damage numbers, round down). This technique can defeat you. You cannot take more damage than you have hp (ie if you have 1 hp and your bond takes 100 dmg, they take 99, you take 1).
