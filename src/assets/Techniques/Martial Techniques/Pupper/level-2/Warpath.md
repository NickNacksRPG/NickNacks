Your momentum empowers you. You deal additional damage based on how far you move before your first [[melee attack]] you make during your turn. This damage is 2 if you move 40 feet before attacking, 4 if you move 80 and 6 if you move 160.

Distance you [[Teleportation|teleport]] does not increase your damage.
