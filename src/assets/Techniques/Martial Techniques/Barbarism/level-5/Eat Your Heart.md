Plunge your hand into a creature and attempt pull out its still beating heart. If the creature is [[defeated]], this automatically happens. If it is not, it takes an additional 4d8 (18) damage. If after the attack is resolved, the creature is defeated you tear out their heart.

When you successfully tear out a creature's heart, you take a bite out of it and gain 10 [[temp hp]]. Strong hearts provide an even greater benefit. If the creature is not a [[minion]], you also gain +2 [[STR]] for 1 minute. This increase in strength is a [[Buff]] and does not stack with itself.

Losing its heart is usually enough to instantly kill a creature. If a creature does not have a heart, you cannot eat it, gaining the techniques secondary effects. A successful [[+Monster Lore]] roll clues you in on the creature's heartiness. [[Undead]] and [[Constructs]] are the most common creatures without hearts. Weird creatures like [[Plants]] and [[Aberrations]] generally have something equivalent.
