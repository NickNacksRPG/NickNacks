Level 1 [[Barbarism]]
Tags: -
Action: Swift

| Range          | -       |
| -------------- | ------- |
| Target/[[AoE]] | Self    |
| Duration       | 1 round |
| Save           | -       |
Start twitching with rage locking eyes with any who dare aggress against you. When you activate this technique, your turn ends. The first creature that deals damage to you until the start of your next turn draws your ire and you can move 30 ft to [[threaten]] it. You have [[advantage]] on attack against this creature until the end of your next turn.
