Level 4 [[Barbarism]]
Tags: -
Action: [[Special Attack]]

| Range          | Reach      |
| -------------- | ---------- |
| Target/[[AoE]] | 1 creature |
| Duration       | -          |
| Save           | -          |
Roll a [[brute force]] check to tear off a piece of a wall, rock or other sturdy object from a structure. Make a [[thrown]] attack against a creature that deals (your athletics roll) damage (typically crush, but can change based on object). If you completely collapse the object such that it falls upon someone, it also deals [[poise]] damage equal to your athletics roll.
