<span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Note</span> - Attack must be made against a [[DD]] creature.

Make a [[Melee Attack]] that deals crush damage instead of its usual damage. [[On-hit]] the creature is knocked out, effectively inflicting the [[asleep]] condition (fort negates). The creature wakes up naturally within 1 minute.

- [[Minion]] creatures do not get a saving throw. 
- [[Boss]] creatures are [[Immunity|immune]]. 
- Creatures whose [[LvL]] is higher than yours get [[advantage]].
