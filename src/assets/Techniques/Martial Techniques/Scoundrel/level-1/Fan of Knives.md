No matter how deep the cavity search, you always have 2 non-magical [[Light Weapon|light weapons]] on your person with the [[thrown]] property. They are in your voluminous hair, fat folds or keistered in less comfortable places...You can draw these weapons as a free action any number of times each turn if your hand(s) are empty.

Gain access to the following [[special attack]], usable any number of times per combat.
<span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">Fan of Knives </span>- Throw up to 3 knives at up to 3 creatures within 10 feet of each other. Ignore attack penalties from attacking creatures in the second [[Range Increments|range increment]].

You also always have an incomplete thief's tool [[Tools|kit]] in your back pocket only suitable for unlocking things in the form of a single lockpick.

Your bonus knives cannot be looted, and disappear shortly after being separated from you.
