*Trade rate of fire/movement for increased accuracy.*
<span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">Requirements</span> -

Carefully line up a [[ranged attack]] and increase the action required to make it to a [[FRA]]. Gain +2 attack and ignore the [[Attack]] penalty for shooting in the second [[Range Increments|range increment]].

Can also be used with standard action attacks, such as spells or [[Bender]]'s elemental blast, you can increase the action requirement from standard to a FRA to gain the +2 bonus to attack.
