*You convert your talent in a particular skill into a way to generate revenue. Maybe you are a professional athlete and use [[+Athletics]], or you are a doctor that uses [[+Medicine]].* 


<span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">Requirements </span>- 

Choose any skill. You can use your bonus to this skill as though it was [[+Profession]] for determining how much income you can make using [[Hard Work]]. See [[Economics]] for more details.


<span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Dev Note - </span>
I understand that this feat might seem weird. I have to take a feat to make money with my skill where I make potions? The idea is that profession is the "core" skill when it comes to making money, and that's basically all it does. This feat is a middle ground that allows people to make money with their gigabuffed skills while also not completely deleting profession because it requires a feat.
