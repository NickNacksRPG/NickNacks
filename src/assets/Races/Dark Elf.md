| <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">Dark Elves</span> | Dark elves are underground dwelling elves that have a long history of desiring more. Hundreds of years ago, there was an entire society of subterranean elves that mostly kept to themselves. Three royal families took it upon themselves to seize control of lesser tribes and force them to work towards their own desire: plunge the surface world into darkness. They were unsuccessful, and banished to [[The Shadow Realm]].<br><br>The tribes made a comeback and almost succeeded in consuming the sun in darkness, bringing about [[4. The Age of Darkness\|The Age of Darkness]]. In the modern era, Dark Elves are hunted by the Storm elves, who provide bounties for pairs of their ears.<br><br>Dark Elves are generally split into three tribes, named after the family line that rules them.<br>[[Shi'Sara]] - The dark elves that hail from the underground beneath the [[Land of Frost]]. They are adept alchemists and cryomancers. They specialize in the art of [[fleshwarping]]: permanently changing creatures into monsters using [[Alchemy]]<br>[[Shi'Zen]] - Hailing from the underground beneath the [[Land of Fire]], they make adept pyromancers and psychics.<br>[[Shi'Sulm]] - Hailing from the underground beneath the [[Land of Earth]], they are trained in the druidic arts, and in particular, form close bonds with insects.<br><br>Because of the far reaching influence of these three tribes, most dark elves are assumed to be part of one, and therefore malicious on sight. |
