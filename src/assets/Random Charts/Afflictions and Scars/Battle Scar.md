When a creature is thrown upon death's door and manages to survive. Every 5 stacks of [[Doom]] you accumulate, you gain a battle scar. Scars are permanent physical effects that generally shouldn't affect gameplay. It's more of a character feel thing. Feel free to think of your own, but here are some examples. Finer detail applied as appropriate for the situation.

| #   | Name         | Description                                                                                                                          |
| --- | ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | Barren       | You can no longer reproduce through traditional means due to nonfunctional organs.                                                   |
| 2   | Scars (face) | You have some scars across your face that are nearly impossible to cover up.                                                         |
| 3   | Scars (body) | You have enormous scars somewhere along your body, representing nearly severed limbs, extreme puncture wounds or giant dragon claws. |
| 4   | Pirate       | You lose one of your eyes completely, or one is damaged beyond repair.                                                               |
| 5   | Bald         | Through extreme burns, acid splashes or stress, much or all of your hair falls out and doesn't return, including face/body hair.     |
| 6   | Branded      | The creature that almost killed you left its mark on you.                                                                            |
| 7   | Missing      | A non-essential piece of you is missing, like a little finger or the tip of your tail.                                               |
| 8   | Crooked      | Something about you is uneven, causing you to limp slightly, or your body to lean to one side as its natural state.                  |
| 9   | Gray         | Your harrowing experience weighs heavy on you, causing your overall complexion to decrease in intensity, closer to gray.             |
| 10  | Crunched     | Your body is compacted, causing you to hunch over                                                                                    |
The [[Regenerate]] technique can remove most scars. Similarly, creatures with [[Regeneration]] cannot gain scars.
