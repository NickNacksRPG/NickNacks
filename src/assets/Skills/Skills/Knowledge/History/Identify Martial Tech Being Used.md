
<span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">DC 15+tech lvl, no action</span>
You must be able to clearly see or hear the creature in order to identify a [[Technique Rules|technique]] that they are casting. Useful for determining whether or not you want to [[Buckler|parry]] something, or figuring out what technique was used that has no immediate visible effect.

If you have [[Technique Point|technique points]] invested in a tech tree, you do not need to roll to identify what they do.
