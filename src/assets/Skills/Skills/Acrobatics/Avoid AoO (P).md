Whenever you are targeted with an [[AoO|Attack of Opportunity]] provoked from [[strafing]] or leaving an enemies [[Threaten|threatened]] zone, you can roll acrobatics as an instant action. Acrobatics is rolled against all attacking creatures’ reflex saves. The DC of their reflex save is equal to your acrobatics roll. If they succeed, your movement [[AoO|Provokes]] as normal. If you succeed, your movement is [[elusive]] against them.

If you later provoke more attacks during your turn in this way against new creatures, you must roll another acrobatics check.
