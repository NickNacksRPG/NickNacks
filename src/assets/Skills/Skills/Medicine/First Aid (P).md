As a standard action you can cure [[+Status]] ailments of a creature within reach. You can remove all [[sickened]] stacks, [[Bleeding|bleed]], [[blurred vision]] and [[blinded]]. The DC of removing any of these conditions is the DC of the ability that inflicted them. If they have no DC, then assume the class/offense trait DC. When you succeed in providing first aid to heal a condition, the creature heals [[LvL]] HP.

Performing first aid without a first aid [[Tools|kit]] imposes a -5 penalty.
