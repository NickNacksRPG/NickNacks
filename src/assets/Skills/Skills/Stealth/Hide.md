As long as you have [[Total Cover]], [[Partial Concealment]] or [[Total Concealment]], you can attempt to hide from your enemies as an immediate action. You remain hidden until no longer have total cover/concealment, you cast a spell, use a special ability, attack or someone spots you. When you attack a creature after hiding from them, you must roll a [[+Stealth]] check. If you beat their perception, they are [[DD]] until the end of your turn.
