As a swift action, roll deception against a creature within reach. DC (10+target's [[LvL]]) or (10+target's [[+Insight]] bonus) whichever is higher. If successful, your opponent is [[DD]] until the end of your turn, or until after your first attack is resolved (or two if [[Dual Wielding]]). 

This is to allow offensive (bigger attack, no parrying etc) and defensive (avoid AoO, deny retaliation strikes) uses for feint.
