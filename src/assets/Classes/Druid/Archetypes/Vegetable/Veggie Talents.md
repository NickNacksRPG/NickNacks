<span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">2 - Brambleback</span>
You grow pointier.
Your vine attacks inflict a 2d6 [[Bleeding|bleed]] effect [[on-hit]], or on a successful [[poise]] break.

Your [[Reach|natural reach]] with your vines increases by 5 ft.

<span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">2 - Mossy</span>
You gain [[camouflage]] in settings with abundant vegetation.

<span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">4 - Hitchhikers</span> [[Recharge]] [[Scrying]]
1/day with physical contact, or [[On-hit]], you can leave behind some annoying seeds that cling to clothing/fur. At any point after, you can [[Scrying|scry]] on this creature, seeing and hearing as though you were present. When you do so, the seeds wither and die.

A creature gets a check to notice the hitchhikers each day using [[+Perception]] (using your class DC or 10+[[+Stealth]] bonus). A creature can also just change clothes/bathe and naturally rid themselves of these annoying seeds, killing them in the process.

<span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">4 - Carnivorous Vines</span>
Your vines grow little mouths at the end, or something to that effect.
Their base damage increases to 2d6.

<span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">10 - Autonomous Vines</span>
Your vine attacks [[threaten]] their entire natural reach when it is not your turn.
