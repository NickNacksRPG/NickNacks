Divine protectors are incredibly frustrating healers that not only excel at healing wounds, but in preventing them altogether through the use of divine shields.

<span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Replaces </span>
- Your Healing Touch loses the [[overheal]] tag and cannot grant temp hp.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Technique </span>
You gain a technique point in the [[Protector]] tree at this level and every 2 levels after.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Divinity</span>
Gain a [[Divine Shield]] that naturally reforms after not taking damage for 1 minute. As a standard action you can refresh your divine shield. As a standard action, you can give an ally within reach a divine shield. These shields last 10 minutes.

Any creature with a divine shield is treated as being within your [[Reach|natural reach]].
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Blaze of Glory (Su)</span> [[recharge]]
When you are [[defeated]], you go out in a blaze of glory. All your allies within 60 ft (including yourself) are immediately granted a divine shield.

This can only happen once a minute.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">2 - Divine Retribution</span> [[Holy]]
Whenever an enemy pops a divine shield, they take holy damage equal to your Healing Touch healing.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">6 - Front Lines</span>
While you have a divine shield, your actions are [[elusive]].
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">10 - Rally</span>
Movement of allies (except you) within ??? is [[elusive]].
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">11 - Divine Army</span>
Creatures you heal with Channel Energy or Healing Touch gain a divine shield.
