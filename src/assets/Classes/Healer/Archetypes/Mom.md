Moms exist to support their team outside of battle more than they do inside the battle. Moms have some weird tricks up her sleeve that let them do some unusual things to get people to change their ways.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Technique </span>
You gain a technique point in the [[Protector]] tree at this level and every 2 levels after.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Snacks</span> [[Healing]] [[Mana Burn]] [[Su]]
By spending 1 hour preparing, and a dash of the secret ingredient, ~~mana~~ love, Mom can make some tasty treats. Each one requires her to mana burn, which she can only recover from if the snacks are eaten or destroyed. They can be used with the [[Use Potion]] or [[Administer Potion]] actions and provide Healing Touch's healing. The main benefit being she can hand them out to others for later consumption.

Each snack is 1 [[Inventory|inventory slot]].
The food can eventually go bad, causing their supernatural benefits and mana burn to disappear.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Emotional Support (healing)</span>
You can remove any tier 1 affliction with 1 hour of coddling.

You gain a unique T1 affliction (see [[Afflictions]] for more details). Other moms cannot remove this affliction with emotional support.

| Name      | Effect                               | Removal Method |
| --------- | ------------------------------------ | -------------- |
| Mom'd out | Cannot use emotional support feature | Standard       |
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Good Boy Points</span> [[Curse]]
Gain access to [[Simp]] as an [[Innate Technique]], usable at will. The creature IS compelled to do what Mom asks. The tasks you can command these cursed individuals are limited however. The creatures must atone for their wrongdoings, they must do nice things for people and they must bestow gifts, each instance granting a point. When a creature earns 3 "good boy points" they are freed from their curse.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Call Me</span> [[Recharge]]
Creatures that have proven they are good boys (see Good Boy Points) can innately use the upcast version of [[Sending]] 1/day, but only to contact mom. Mom can also any such creature (1/day each). They cannot cut off the conversation.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Professional Mom</span>
You get a unique [[+Profession]] proficiency called Mom with a +4 bonus. You can use to to perform any type of mom-y activities, such as daycare services for children. Tangential/stereotypical features of momming, such as cooking and cleaning can use this skill, but do not receive the +4 bonus.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">2 - Hug</span> [[Su]]
By spending 1 mana as a standard action, you can grapple an ally to put them in [[stasis]] until the end of your next turn and remove all [[shaken]] stacks.

When you successfully [[Grapple]] an enemy, they are [[pacified]] for 1 round and the grapple immediately ends.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">2 - Lost and Found (recharge)</span>
Mom has a 6th sense for objects of significance, and often randomly picks them up for safekeeping. You can procure a specific item a creature once owned that someone mentions they misplaced, so long as you have been to a location it could be reasonably located.
Ex. "Oh you guys went into Old Man's Cave? I remember going there once. Lost my wedding ring there."
"This wouldn't happen to be it would it?"

You can't use it to proactively search for something, like a secret entrance, or a sacred artifact, unless an ancient resurrected hero is missing their sword and it was last seen in a place you’ve been (and it wasn't closely guarded). This doesn't let you retrieve items that are in someone else's possession and the DM can choose to deny particularly unlikely circumstances.

Recharges every Monday.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">4 - Mommy Energy</span>
You exude a palpable aura of love. Creature's find it difficult to harm you. Creatures who attempt to harm you must redirect their aggression for the remainder of their turn (will negates). This does not consume the action, but it does force them to alter it, such as attacking another creature, or excluding you from an AoE.

Once a creature passes their save, or you deal damage, mom is on the menu and this aura is gone.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">4 - Blessing of Restoration (Su) (healing)</span> 
You recover from all stacks of [[Drained|drain]] every time you rest, regardless of conditions.

As a standard action, you can touch a creature to absorb ([[LvL]]) stacks of drain from them and you become drained an equal amount. A creature can only be affected by this once each week.
