[[Shira, the She-Devil]]'s chosen, able to mimic her ability to summon spectral tendrils. The animancer uses these tendrils in order to deal any damage, using their spells in order to debilitate enemies. The deeper into progression the more powerful and versatile the tendrils become. In addition, the animancer can create a cult following of sorts that increases their influence.

## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Techniques</span>
You have [[magical specialization]].
You gain 1 technique point at level 1, then another point every 2 levels after. These can be used on any magical technique.
In addition, you automatically memorize every [[Shira]] technique without spending memory.

Uniquely, whenever you cast any spell that deals [[+HP]] damage, even as an [[innate technique]] gained from another source, <span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">it does not deal any hp damage</span>. This bypasses the usual rule of negating [[+Status|status effects]] when they deal 0 damage, allowing spells like [[Fire Blast]] or [[Rainbow Beam]] to be taken for utility, even if you miss out on the damage they deal.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Animancy</span> [[Su]] [[Companion]]
As a standard action, or a swift action for 1 [[mana]], you can summon an immobile medium sized spectral tendril attached to a surface within 30 ft of you. These tendrils share your [[ability scores]], [[LvL]] are [[incorporeal]], have [[Caster Level|CL]] hp, and automatically fail any saving throw. They have 10 ft of [[reach]], and [[threaten]], expending your [[AoO]]s.

They deal 2d6+[[Caster Level|CL]] crush damage with their attacks.

When summoned outside of combat, tendrils are [[invisible]] until they act. This has the [[subtle]] tag. In order to [[DD|Surprise]] an enemy with tendril attacks, you must use [[+Stealth]] or [[Create Diversion]].

Summoning a tendril when your companion slots are already full thanks to tendrils causes a previous tendril to disappear. Unlike most companion creatures, tendrils do not get a turn of their own and rely on other class features (or [[AoO]]s) to attack.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Skill</span>
Gain [[+Deception]] as a bonus skill with a +4 bonus. Gain [[Signature Skill]] for deception. 
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Command Tendrils: Attack</span>
Speak a command in infernal to your tendrils as a standard action, commanding them to strike. They attack a creature you designate, or different creatures if all tendrils cannot reach the same creature. You can have one of the tendrils use a [[special attack]] you can perform when using command attack.

Your tendrils also automatically attack any creature you inflict a [[+Status|status effect]] on with a [[spell]].
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Sphere of Influence</span> [[Curse]] [[Scrying]]
You gain the ability to inflict a unique curse on conscious willing creatures with at least -2 [[INT]]. To inflict this curse, the creature must agree to serve you, and you must establish physical contact, inflicting a mark upon them. They are permanently [[charmed]] and gain [[resistance]] to other charm effects. You can scry on any creature charmed by you.

For every creature inflicted with this curse that is not level 0, gain 1 max HP (up to a maximum of 4*[[LvL]]).

To remove this curse, the creature with the curse must scrub at the mark while cursing your name. When this happens, you can immediately scry them for 1 minute.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Deific Boons</span>
You start play with 10 [[Deific Boons|GBP]] for [[Shira, the She-Devil]] unless your [[WIS]] is -1 or lower. 
You gain infernal as a bonus [[Languages|language]].
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">2 - Elongate</span>
The [[Reach|natural reach]] of your tendrils increases by 5 ft. This range increase happens again at level 4, 6, 8 and 10 (to a maximum of 35 ft).
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">2 - Command Tendrils: Defend</span>
Speak a command in infernal to your tendrils as a standard action, commanding them to protect. They act as though taking the [[total defense]] action for a creature you designate, or different creatures if all tendrils cannot reach the same creature. For every tendril past the first protecting the same creature, increase the [[DR|DRA]] granted by this action by 2.

Instead of the creature targeted being unable make AoOs, the tentacles are unable to do so while defending.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">4 - Command Tendrils: Reposition</span> [[Elusive]]
Speak a command in infernal to your tendrils as a standard action, commanding them to shuffle allies around. Each tendril can move an ally anywhere within their reach. You can even chain together movement between multiple tentacles "passing" the same ally.

You can treat defeated enemies as allies.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">6 - Rumormonger</span>
You can attempt to spread a rumor throughout a small town or larger settlement as a [[+Downtime]] activity by attempting a deception check. The difficulty depends on the size, ranging from 10 for a hamlet to 30 for a metropolis (see [[Populations]]), though this difficulty may be modified if the population as a whole tends towards skepticism or insanity.

It takes a week for a rumor to spread. If you succeed by 5 or more, the time is reduced to 3 days. If you fail by 5 or more, the rumor mutates into a competing theory.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">8 - Spawn (Su)</span>
Whenever you [[defeat]] an enemy, you can cause a new tendril to spawn at its location.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">10 - Command Tendrils: Revenge</span>
Speak a command in infernal to your tendrils as a standard action, commanding them to strike back at attackers. Designate yourself or any ally. Whenever the designated creature takes HP damage or is poise broken from a direct attack (ie not things like [[Bleeding|bleed]]), all threatening tentacles can attack the aggressor.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">11 - Animaster</span>
Whenever you summon a tendril, you instead summon 2 adjacent to each other. You can break the usual companion cap of 2, increasing it to 3, as long as at least one of these companions is a tendril.
