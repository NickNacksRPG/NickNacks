## [[Crust]]
Mechanics are engineers that put their heart and soul into their gadgets and gizmos, preferring one specific creation that they pilot into battle.

## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Techniques</span>
You gain 2 Technique Points at level 1, then another point every 2 levels after.
These Technique Points can be used on any tree, though [[Spell|spells]] require a way to give your Mech the ability to qualify as a [[Staff Component|spell focus]].
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Skill</span>
You get [[Engineering]] as a bonus skill with a +4 bonus.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Greatest Creation</span>
You start off with a specific type of mech, determined by archetype. The variety tends to come from how integrated it is into the mechanics themselves, such as a vehicle they pilot or a suit that augments their bodies.

When you are [[Defeated]], this mech becomes unusable and requires repairing. This requires a (30-Engineering roll) minutes (minimum 1) and appropriate tools. If you ever lose this mech, you can recreate it in (30-engineering roll) days (minimum 1) and 200g of materials like metals and leather seats.

See [[+Magical Conduits]] on how you can enhance your mech. Instead of the traditional method of forming a conduit, you instead integrate the weapons directly into the mech. When you do so, you directly upgrade your existing weapons/armor using the loot as a material.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Supercharge</span>
Mechs have many powerful abilities that require you to gather power as a [[Free Action]], allowing you to use one of the <span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Supercharge</span> abilities on your next turn. This always has some kind of distinct audio and visual quality, such as revving engines, glowing chassis, or steam erupting from the mech's nooks. You automatically fail [[+Stealth]] checks when Supercharging, and creatures get +20 [[+Perception]] to notice you when Supercharging.
 
While Supercharging, you cannot move. You can choose to end the Supercharge and start it again at a later point on your turn to avoid this. The intention is to supercharge at the end of turn, then because you are locked in place at the start of your next turn, you can decide to spend your supercharge, or to end it so that way you can move and act normally.

Because Supercharging is an aggressive action that is easily noticed, it will almost always result in combat starting when the action is taken, making it infeasible to use a Supercharge ability on the first round.

<span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">You cannot Supercharge and use a Supercharge ability on the same turn.</span> You must alternate turns between Supercharging and using a move that consumes the effect.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Augment</span>
Gain 4 Augments, two of which use the Charge feature. Augments are customizable features that can give you new abilities, add additional features to your mech's limbs and enhance your physical abilities.

Gain an additional Augment each even level.

Some augments have level restrictions denoted by a number in (parenthesis).
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">2 - Lock On</span>
You can choose a foe within 60 ft of as a [[Swift Action]] to hone in on. Your attacks have [[Seeking]] and +2 attack against this foe until you dismisses this ability (a [[Free Action]]). 

You automatically know the position of the creature, even if they turn [[Invisible]] or gain [[Total Cover]]. This ability does not negate cover for the purposes of attacking this foe, but you are still aware of the foe's position until it moves at least 120 ft away from you.

You only Lock On to one creature at a time.
