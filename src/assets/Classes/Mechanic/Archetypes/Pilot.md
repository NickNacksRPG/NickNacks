Pilots hop into their mechs and drive them around.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Mech Basics</span>
Mechs must be one size category larger than you to use. In order for another creature to use your mech, they must roll a DC (20+your engineering bonus) to use it. Otherwise it is a very fancy hunk of metal to them.

You are effectively treated as [[Mount|mounted]] on your mech while driving it. Any damage it takes depletes your hp and any effects targeting the mech target you (such as a [[Charming Gaze]] spell). Any attacks it makes use your stats, etc. It is not a separate creature, but an extension of yourself. 

The only exceptions are
- Use the [[AC]] value of your mech while mounted in it. This can alter your [[Poise]] as well.
- You use the listed [[Carry Weight]] of the mech. You do not count against this weight, but your equipment and items do.

While piloting, your own arms are occupied. You use the Mech's arms instead. By default, your mech has two arms and two legs. Its arms can perform [[Unarmed]] attacks, wield [[+Weapons]], but also use a pincer [[Natural Attack]]. 
<span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Pincer: </span>2d6 [[Crush]] Damage

The Mech may change what its arms are at the start of its turn, similarly and simultaneously to [[Swap Item]]. Its arms are part of its body, and thus cannot be [[Disarm|Disarmed]] like a regular weapon can.

A Mech weighs about a ton. You can build spare sets of parts ahead of time (in the time it would normally take to make a Mech) to quickly replace a lost Mech in the time it would normally take to repair one.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Emergency Exit</span>
At any point, you can eject from your mech as an instant action and appear in its space. Remounting requires a swift action, but cannot be done in the same turn you exit. Emergency exit needs to be manually recharged as a standard action while mounted to use again.
