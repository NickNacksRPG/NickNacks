## [[Crust]]
You know the greatest secrets of them all. The kind of secrets that the masters refuse to teach in other schools of martial arts. You have mastered the power of suggestion to such an extent that you appear to manipulate psychic energy, stave off debilitating disease and defeat your opponents without even touching them. In reality, your confidence is just so overpowering that people cannot help but kneel.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Master of Deception</span>
Get the [[+Deception]] skill with a +4 bonus. You have [[advantage]] on deception checks.

By asking leading questions and making accusations, you can roll a deception check in order to get someone to say something that they know out loud. You must talk to someone for at least a few minutes before you're able to do this.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - No Touch Knockout</span> [[Special Attack]]
With an astonishing flourish, you can [[defeat]] a creature instantly without even dealing any damage. Roll a deception check instead of an attack roll. If your check is greater than their current [[+HP]], the creature is instantly defeated. This can never kill a creature.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Reputation</span> [[Fear]]
Anybody can recognize you with a DC 10 [[INT]] [[Ability Check]]. With the [[+Local]] skill, no roll is required. Your reputation is a healthy mix of fact and fiction.
When you roll initiative, and you are not hidden, you can announce your presence. Any [[minion]] creature is automatically [[Frightened]] by you for the first round. Choose one additional creature to focus your grandstanding on, making them also frightened for 1 round (will negates).

Attacks
- Poison hand pressure point nonsense
- No touch knockout
- Psychic powers to block/overpower people
- The ability to generate photos of the creature being defeated through distracting them
- Preemptive strike on people will to fight, based sole-y on reputation alone
- "Miracle Kneel" The ability to feign defeat to get out of danger, or lul the enemy into a false sense of security that allies can exploit
- Taunting
- Seemingly immune to all status effects, but really you just delay them

Skills
- Extreme talent, but only in contests against others
- Use deception for knowledge by asking leading questions
