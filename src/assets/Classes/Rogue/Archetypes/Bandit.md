An expert thief, a blur of shadows around the battlefield taking everything their opponents have and stepping back before they realize what happened.
Your very shadow steps out to reach into people's pockets when they aren't looking.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Shadow</span> [[Su]]
You have a sentient, autonomous manifestation of dark magic and inner corruption, acting as your loyal, silent partner. It appears as your shadow, sticking by your side and manipulating objects alongside you. It grants you a proficiency and a +4 bonus in [[+Sleight of Hand]], and allows you to attempt to steal from anyone regardless of their attentiveness. Your Cunning Actions have +10 reach when assisted by it.

You can choose to make this shadow appear or disappear once per round as a [[Free Action]], but you must use Sleight of Hand normally while it isn't helping you.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Pickpocket</span>
[[On-hit]] and successful [[Poise]] breaks drop coins around the target in their space (or one you attack from with larger creatures). When collected they are worth (10*[[LvL]]) gold. If you collect them, it's worth double. You can collect the coins as a free action, but other creatures must spend an action to collect or otherwise move the money. Before it's looted or moved, the coins are <span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Blood Money</span>. 

This ability represents taking something away from your opponent with each hit. If you use it while sparring or against allies to "farm" money, they may need that money back to replace the golden tooth they lost, jewelry you pilfered, the kidney you stole etc.

[[GM]] discretion.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Cunning Action: Steal</span>
You can use Cunning Action to perform [[Thievery]]. You can use Steal to produce <span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Blood Money</span> once per creature, in addition to taking something from them.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Money Magic </span>
Gain on of the "money magic" abilities below. Gain another one at level 2, 6, and 10.

All of these abilities are [[Su|supernatural]] and kill blood money to varying degrees, making you unable to collect it.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">2 - Band of Thieves</span> [[Companion]] 
You can easily find and hire goons to call upon for aid. These are [[Humanoids]] with half your [[+HP]] (including when summoned), and +2 [[DEX]] and [[LUK]]. They are [[Minion|minions]] of half your level. They have no other abilities or statistics of their own (no techniques, feats, class features, etc) other than a [[+Sleight of Hand]] and [[+Stealth]] bonus that is (your bonus-4).

You may equip them as you please, and Summon them as a Cunning Action as if by a [[Summoning Contract]]. They take their turn on your turn - the player controls them.

They can create <span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Blood Money</span> for you using the same methods as you.

The first two goons are free, but additional goons or replacing goons costs 500g each. 

You can train specific creatures to be your goons and be summon-ready, such as [[Beasts]] or friendly NPCs, but their stats change to match a hired goon regardless. This is mostly for flavor.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">4 - Grand Larceny</span>
When you use the Steal action, you can Steal from everything in reach.

## <span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 80)">Money Magic</span>

<span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">Coin Explosion</span>
As an action, explode ALL of your Blood Money, dealing your 2d8 (9) Crush and Poise damage to enemies within 10ft of a coin (Reflex halves). Getting hit by multiple explosions increases the damage by d8 (4) per additional coin they're hit by.

<span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">Cunning Action: Golden Opportunity</span>
Make one specific <span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Blood Money</span> particularly enticing by dropping another 5x its value into the pile as a standard action. This money becomes particularly enticing to enemies within 5 ft of it, forcing them to spend their standard action to pick it up (will negates). Minion creatures get no saving throw, and those that aren't summoned will attempt to escape with the money.

Once a creature picks up the money, it cannot entice anybody else.

<span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">Cunning Action: Misdirection</span>
As a swift action, cause a [[Darting Duplicate]] to appear from Blood Money within your [[Reach|natural reach]].
