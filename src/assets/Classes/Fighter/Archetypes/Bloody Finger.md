## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Bleed Out</span>
Any piercing or slashing weapon you wield gains the [[Bloodied]] property without taking an [[+Magic Weapon Properties|enchantment slot]]. 
[[On-hit]] with a bleed weapon (even if it does no damage), keep a running tally of how many times the creature was hit. Each applies a stack of "<span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">hemorrhage</span>."  [[Crit|Crits]] apply 2 stacks. Hemorrhage stacks are visible on the creatures body and last until you rest.

Even if a creature is immune to [[bleeding]], it can still gain stacks of hemorrhage.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Hemorrhage</span> [[Su]]
Sheathe your weapon(s) as a standard action, causing creatures you attacked since your last rest. Creatures that have <span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">hemorrhage</span> stacks (no matter the distance) lose all stacks and take d8 [[True Damage]] per stack.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Bloodied Finger</span>
One of the bloody finger's fingers is always a crimson red. It can be used to write in a bloody ink.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Weeb Training</span>
You can dual wield one handed weapons using [[DEX]] or [[STR]] as long as the weapon has a Japanese name. 
If you wield a Japanese weapon in two hands, it applies 2 <span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">hemorrhage</span> stacks on hit, or 4 on a crit.
By getting an [[Anime]] to authenticate your sword (for 10% of its base price), you can get an inscription on the sword to make it count as a weeb weapon.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">2 - Delayed Doom</span>
As a free action, you can decide to make your attacks deals 0 apparent damage. Roll damage anyway and take note of it, applying stacks of <span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">hemorrhage</span> as normal but forfeiting all other on hit effects. The next time you use hemorrhage, any creature you struck with delayed doom takes all of this stored damage as additional true damage.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">4 - Invasion</span> [[Sigil]]
You can inscribe your name, or a symbol of significance to you, on a surface using your bloodied finger. When a creature triggers the sigil, you get a glimpse of the surrounding area briefly. Anytime it is triggered, you can choose to crawl out of the sigil as though you were using a [[Summoning Contract]] to summon yourself, causing the sigil to disappear. You can stay summoned this way for 5 minutes before you return to your body.

You cannot use this feature while you have [[summoning sickness]]. If you create a sigil while you already have one, the old one disappears.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">8 - Rivers of Blood (Su)</span> [[Recharge]]
As a standard action, slash the air rapidly, causing many bloody projectiles to fly out in a 30 ft cone in front of you. Creatures in the area take 5d8+[[LvL]] (22+lvl) slash or pierce damage and 4  <span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">hemorrhage</span> stacks (reflex halves damage and hemorrhage stacks).

This ability recharges when you use your hemorrhage ability against a creature with 5 or more hemorrhage stacks, or when you rest.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">10 - Blood Scent</span>
Gain [[Scent]] (120 ft) and [[Seeking]] against wounded creatures. 
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">11 - Lord of Blood</span>
Whenever a creature takes bleed damage within 30 ft of you, they gain a <span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">hemorrhage</span> stack.
