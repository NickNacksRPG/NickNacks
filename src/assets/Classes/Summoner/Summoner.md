## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Techniques</span>
You have [[magical specialization]].
You gain 1 technique point at level 1, then another point every 2 levels after. These can be used on any magical technique.
In addition, you automatically memorize every technique that has the [[Companion]] tag without spending memory, limited by your level (see [[Technique Progression]]).
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Commander</span>
Gain a [[+Teamwork Feats|teamwork feat]]. You automatically share all of your teamwork feats with your companion creatures.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Soul Link</span>
When you would be [[defeated]], you can negate the effect entirely by spending 1 mana as an immediate action. When you do, you choose to instead have one of your companion creatures be defeated.
