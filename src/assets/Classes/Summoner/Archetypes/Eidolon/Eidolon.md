Eidolons are extraplanar entities bound to a mortal via a complex ritual that can typically only be performed once in a lifetime. This page tells you how they work. Eidolons are quite complicated, and are basically like playing two characters at once.

# <span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 80)">Basic Stats</span>
<span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">LvL</span> - An eidolon's [[LvL]] matches yours
<span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">Ability Scores</span> - An eidolon gets a similar stat array that a player gets. See [[Ability Scores]]
<span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">Type</span> - Eidolons can be either [[Celestials]] or [[Fiends]], affecting what types of abilities it can gain.
<span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">Mana</span> - An eidolon does not get its own [[mana]] pool, and shares its mana with you. You gain (or lose) mana based on the eidolon's [[INT]] score. You are both treated as one creature when calculating the technique cap per turn (see [[Technique Rules|techniques]]).
<span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">Memory</span> - An eidolon does not get its own [[Memory]], and shares its memory slots with you. You gain (or lose) memory based on the eidolon's [[INT]] score. All techniques you memorize, the eidolon also memorizes (including [[Prepared]] techniques).
<span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">Other Progression</span> - Eidolons get a feat every odd level, but do not get a technique point on even levels.
# <span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 80)">Death and Defeat</span>
A summoned eidolon cannot be killed.  When it is [[defeated]], it is immediately sent back to its [[-Planes|Plane]] of origin. In order to summon it again after being defeated, you must [[Drained|drain]] 5.

If the summoner is defeated or dies, the eidolon disappears at the end of its next turn (or current turn if somehow you died on your own turn).

## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Multiattack</span>
Make 2 attacks, one of which must be a [[natural attack]].
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Evolution</span>
Eidolons start play with 5 [[Polymorph List|adaptation points]] they can use in the [[Polymorph List]], and gains an additional one each level after 1. You can pick anything in the general tab. If they are a fiend, you can choose upgrades from the fiend section. If they are a celestial, you can choose upgrades from the celestial section.

As a bonus, the eidolon comes with a  [[Natural Attack]] that has d8 base damage. Additionally, you must also choose the base shape of an eidolon.

## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Undercover</span> [[Polymorph|poly, true]]
An eidolon can transform into a specific form as a standard action, and remain in this form indefinitely. The transformation matches your race, and they appear similar enough to you that they can be mistaken as a relative, with minor traits reminiscent of their true form. While in this form, they lose access to all of their Evolutions that are not tied to improved senses (ie [[Nightvision]] or [[Scent]]).

The eidolon can transform into their true form as an immediate action.
