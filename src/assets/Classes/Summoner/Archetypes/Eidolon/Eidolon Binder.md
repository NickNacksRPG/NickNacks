Early on in your magical research, you discovered the existence of a particular creature and how to summon them. Through force or or diplomacy, you managed to convince them to sign a contract that bound them to you for life. The creature's power is limited by your own. As you grow more powerful, you are able to summon a more potent version of this eidolon, making it a more accurate representation of the creature you bound when you first started if it was a powerful creature, or giving it completely new abilities if it was not.

## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Skill</span>
Gain [[+Planes]] as a bonus skill with a +4 bonus.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Eidolon</span> [[Su]] [[Companion]]
With a ritual taking (11-[[LvL]] rounds), you can summon a powerful extraplanar creature. At level 11, you can summon your eidolon as a standard action. It lasts indefinitely and disappears when it is [[Defeated]]. You and your eidolon share a mark on each of your faces that are identical, or mirror each other in some way.

More details can be found on the [[Eidolon]] page.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Techniques</span>
You have [[Martial Specialization]]. 
You get 1 technique point at this level and every 2 levels after. You can use these technique points on the [[Pupper]], [[Beastmode]] or [[Cataclysm]] trees.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">2 - Mana Wells</span>
Your [[Mana]] increases by 1 this level and at level 4, 6, 8 and 10.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">2 - Share Senses (Su)</span> [[Scrying]]
If you eidolon is currently summoned and on the same plane as you, you can scry on it.
Your eidolon can also scry on you with the same conditions.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">4 - Shield Master</span> 
While within your eidolon's [[Reach|natural reach]] and the eidolon is not [[Helpless]], [[Dazed]] or otherwise unable to act, you have a +2 bonus to [[+Saving Throw|Saves]].
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">6 - Master's Call (Su)</span> [[Teleportation]]
If your eidolon is currently summoned and on the same plane as you, you can teleport it to any space adjacent to you as a swift action by spending 3 mana.
