Master summoners study one particular type of creature extensively, learning to create temporary copies of creatures to use in battle. The extreme versatility of their summoning feature allows them to have a wide variety of answers to all kinds of problems in or out of combat.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Obsession</span>
Choose one creature type (such as [[Giants]], [[Plants]] or [[Fiends]]). Many of your class teachers are centered around this creature type.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Skill</span>
Gain the [[+Knowledge]] skill associated with your obsession as a bonus skill with a +4 bonus. When you roll [[+Monster Lore]] against this creature type, you always glean at least 1 category.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1 - Summoning</span> [[Su]] [[Companion]]
You can summon creatures that are the subject of your obsession. Summoning is a standard action, placing the creature anywhere within 30 ft of you. Because it is a companion, it can act immediately if summoned in combat. These summons last indefinitely. You [[Mana Burn]] equal to 1+(half a summoned creature's LvL). This mana burn becomes permanent if the creature is defeated. A summoned creature can dismiss itself as a standard action to cause the mana burn to disappear (does not affect current mana).

Summoning has the following limitations...
- One creature you summon using this ability can be (your [[LvL]])-1.
- The other(s) must be a [[Minion]] to you. 
- You cannot summon a [[Boss]] creature
- You can only summon a creature in a specific list on the bestiary, even if a creature has multiple types. For example, if you chose [[Beasts]], you would not be able to summon a [[Yummi]] because its "primary" type is [[Fey]].
- You always summon the same creature. For example if you chose to summon an [[Owl Bear]], it would always be the same one. If a summoned creature is defeated, it cannot be summoned again that day. The creature follows orders, but remembers its past lives.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">2 - Mana Wells</span>
Your [[Mana]] increases by 1 this level and at level 4, 6, 8 and 10.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">11 - Swarm</span>
You can have 3 [[Companion]] creatures.
