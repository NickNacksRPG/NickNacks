## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">Parrying Dagger - Pierce, Light, Parrying (2lbs, 200g)</span>

- You get access to the [[parry]] action, but it takes a standard action
- When you successfully parry a creature, you can choose to either set them to 1 [[poise]], or make an attack against them as an instant action. This attack also gets the +2 bonus from the parrying quality.
