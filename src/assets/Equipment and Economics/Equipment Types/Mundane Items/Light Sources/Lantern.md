## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">50g</span>

Lanterns are metal containers with candles or other light sources inside protected by barriers, usually a sturdy glass. Lanterns give off [[Light Level|Bright Light]] in a 20 ft radius around them, and [[Light Level|Dim Light]] 20 ft past that.

Lanterns are lit as a swift action. They can stay lit up for 8 hours. You can reset this time by replacing the light source inside. 

Lanterns can be held in the hand, or they can be attached to the hip, consuming a [[+Baubles|bauble]] slot. If a creature [[poise]] breaks you, they can choose to instead [[disarm]] you of your lantern, or knock it off your body, deactivating its light.

A lantern requires 1 [[Inventory|inventory slot]], with another dedicated to refills.
Refills cost 25g.

<span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">Special:</span>
Lanterns can also come in a "bullseye" variant with a special lens that amplifies light in a singular direction. They have the area of light narrowed, changing the area that is lit up from a 20 ft radius into a 60 ft cone, with another 60 ft of dim light after that. Because of the higher precision required, bullseye lanterns must always be wielded and cannot be attached at the hip.
