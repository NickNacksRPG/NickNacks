## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">2,000g</span>

Tags: [[Su]], [[Curse]], [[Polymorph]]
_This piece of enchanted wood has been decorated with a handsome pair of goblins along its shaft, one male and one female._

Falls under [[Wands]]

<span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">Benefit(s): </span>
- While wielding the wand, you can increase the [[Caster Level|CL]] of a spell you cast by 2 as a swift action. 
- As a standard action, you can [[Polymorph]] a willing or [[helpless]] creature into a [[goblin]], gaining no special abilities, but changing their size and appearance.

<span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Drawback: </span>
- When you increase the CL of a spell with the wand, the caster automatically transforms into a specific goblin form. Roll a d20. Odds is male, evens is female. Appearance is generally conventionally attractive, resembling their previous appearance (as though a relative). If the caster is already a goblin, it instead targets the nearest creature.
- If you transform someone into a goblin, the same thing happens.
- While a goblin, the creature also cannot be subject to any other kind of polymorph effect.

In order to remove the effects of the wand, a polymorphed creature must [[rest]] while sharing the same bed (or something similar) with another goblin.
