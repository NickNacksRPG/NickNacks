## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">500g (DC 18)</span>

Tags: [[Healing]]
All kinds of plants and animal parts can be used in the creation of healing potions.

Immediately restores 5 hp.

By increasing the DC by 4, you can double the price and increase its healing by 5. This doubling in price/+4 DC is cumulative 
T2 - DC 22 for 1,000g for 10 healing
T3 - DC 26 2,000g for 15 healing
T4 - DC 30 for 4,000g for 20 healing
etc...

<span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">T3 and above potions are far less common due to their high craft DC.</span>
