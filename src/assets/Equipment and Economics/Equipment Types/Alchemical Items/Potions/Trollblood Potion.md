## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">700g, (DC 20)</span>

Tags: [[Healing]]
By scraping off the natural excretions of a troll's skin, trollblood potions can be made.

When consumed, the creature gains [[regeneration]] 1 for 1 minute. This effect is suppressed for 1 round if the creature takes fire/poison damage.
