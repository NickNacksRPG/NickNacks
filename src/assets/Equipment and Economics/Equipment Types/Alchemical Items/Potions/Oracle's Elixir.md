## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">2,200g (DC 24)</span>

Tags: [[Buff]]
Powerful hallucinogens can be mixed with eyes of raptor birds to enhance a wearer's vision to see the unseen. While under the effects of an oracle's elixir, the creatures eyes glow pink.

Grant the benefits of [[Glimpse of Truth]] for 1 minute. After the elixir wears off, the creature has [[Blurred Vision]] for 10 minutes that cannot be removed via [[First Aid (P)|first aid]].
