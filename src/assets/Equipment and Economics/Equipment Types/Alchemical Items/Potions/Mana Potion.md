## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">500g (DC 18)</span>

By melting down mana crystals, they can be converted into a tasty sweet potion that can be addictive.

Immediately restores 1 [[mana]], but you [[mana burn]] 1, reducing your maximum mana. Normally mana burn also reduces current mana, but to keep the math simple, increase current mana by 1 and reduce max by 1.

By increasing the DC by 4, you can double the price and increase its mana restoration and burn by 1. This doubling in price/+4 DC is cumulative.
T2 - DC 22 for 1,000g for +2 mana and burn
T3 - DC 26 2,000g for +3 mana and burn
T4 - DC 30 for 4,000g for +4 mana and burn
etc...

<span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">T3 and above potions are far less common due to their high craft DC.</span>
