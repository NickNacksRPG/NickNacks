## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">200g (DC 10)</span>

Tags: [[Recharge]]
Anti toxin is a liquid that can be drank or directly applied to wounds as a standard action, the type of application matching whatever the caused the toxin. Gives an additional save to end the effects of a [[+Toxins|Toxin]] or [[venom]].
Using multiple antitoxins on the same creature in the same 24 hour period has no effect.
