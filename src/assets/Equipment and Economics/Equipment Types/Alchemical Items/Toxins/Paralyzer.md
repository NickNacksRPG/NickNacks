*This toxin feels like frozen water is pumping through your veins.*

<span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Delivery</span> - Injury
Creature becomes afflicted with the toxin for 3 rounds (fort negates).
Creature becomes clumsy 1 at the start of each of its turns. If it is already clumsy 4, then it becomes [[Entangled]] for 1 round instead.
