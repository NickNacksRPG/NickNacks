## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">400g (DC 16)</span>

Mineral deposits containing salt exposed to powerful magic can sometimes cause the salt to take a blue hue, giving it anti-magic properties. Mages are hesitant to mass produce it for obvious reasons.

When [[thrown]] on a [[spell]] or [[Su]] ability with a [[persistent]] effect, attempt to [[Dispel]] with a +3 bonus.
