Come in several distinct categories based on their typical use case.

The format includes the items name, the DC to craft it and its cost to purchase. [[+Toxins]] also mention a method of administration.

I put alchemical items into different price brackets. One for relatively minor and commonplace items another for powerful items, and the last for things requiring incredibly rare reagents.

Item DC refers to craft DC, which determines the price. The save DC is typically a base amount. Creatures with a higher DC can craft items with a higher DC, adjusting the price accordingly.

Cheap - DC^2-80. Then rounded up to nearest 10.

Common - (DC+5)^2. Then rounded down to nearest 100

Expensive - 1000+(DC+10)^2. Then round up to nearest 100
