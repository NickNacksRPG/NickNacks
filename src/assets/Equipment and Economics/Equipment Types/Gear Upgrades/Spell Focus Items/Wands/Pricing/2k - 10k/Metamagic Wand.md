## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">3,000g+</span>

While wielding a wand, you can increase the [[Caster Level|CL]] of a spell you cast by 1 as a swift action.

A metamagic wand also grants the user the ability to use a particular Metamagic Feat, chosen when the wand is created. If the cast time is a standard action or a [[FRA]], activating the MM feat through the wand requires a swift action in addition to its usual mana cost increase.

The price of a metamagic wand is 3,000g, +3,000g for each additional point of [[INT]] the feat has as a prerequisite. 
Ex.
A [[MM Latent Curse]] wand would cost 3k.
A [[MM Bouncing Spell]] wand would cost 12k.
