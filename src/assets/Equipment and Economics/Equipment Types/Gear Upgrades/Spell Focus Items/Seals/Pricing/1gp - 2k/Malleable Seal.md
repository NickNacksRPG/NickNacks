## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">2,000g</span>

Tags: -

*This paper seal has a simple magic circle inked onto it.*

When placed on the hand, the malleable seal sticks to and almost completely molds to the shape of your hand. A few corners poke out, similar to an old adhesive losing its stickyness. You can wield other equipment in the hand while a malleable seal is stuck to you, but you cannot cast spells using the seal unless the hand is empty.

Malleable seals stay available for use, even through [[Polymorph]] effects that normally absorb your equipment. 

<span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Note - </span>
Even if you have your [[Staff Component|spell focus]], [[Polymorph|poly, baleful]] effects like [[Enfroggen]] usually also prevent you from speaking, making the malleable seal ill suited to defending against them.
