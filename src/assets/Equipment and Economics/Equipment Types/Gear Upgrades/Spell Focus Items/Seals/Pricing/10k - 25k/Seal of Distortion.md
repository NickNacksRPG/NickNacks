## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">12,000g</span>

Tags: [[Su]], [[Teleportation]]

*This seal is a handful of gems that float to the tips of the fingers when held, with a larger gem in the epicenter of the palm.*

Whenever you cast a [[spell]] that includes movement ([[Rushing Winds]]), or requires movement to function ([[Nauseating Trail]]), you can spend 1 mana to place your hand against the ground to cause a copy of the seal to appear onto the ground immediately after casting. Until the end of your next turn, you can teleport to this location as an instant action when you or another creature ends their turn.
