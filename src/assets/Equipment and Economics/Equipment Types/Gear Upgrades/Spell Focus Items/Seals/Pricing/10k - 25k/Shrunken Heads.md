## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">14,000g</span>

Tags: -

*A small group of dried heads all knotted together by hair or strings. Their eyes and mouths are sown shut.*

When crafting shrunken heads, careful consideration should be made of the creature type. Your [[Spell|spells]] gain a +2 [[DC]] against the creature the heads once possessed (ie [[Dragons]], [[Humanoids]]). If the heads are made from creatures with multiple creature types, you choose whatever the primary type is (most [[+Races]] are humanoid first, bestiary creatures are listed under their primary type).
