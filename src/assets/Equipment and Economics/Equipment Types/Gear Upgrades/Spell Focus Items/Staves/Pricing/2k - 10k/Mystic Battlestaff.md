## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">3,000g</span>

This staff can focus beams and blasts into its wood, allowing [[Ranged Attack|ranged]] [[Spell Attack|spell attacks]] to be made as [[Melee Attack|melee]] [[Weapon Attack|weapon attacks]]. If the spell allows multiple attacks, it follows the same targeting restrictions, such as an upcast [[Gloom Bolt]] requiring separate targets for each attack.

See [[staff]] for base stats.
