## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">3,000g</span>

This staff has a mana crystal affixed to its end. The wood the staff is crafted from and the patterns etched into it influences what [[Technique Rules|technique]] tree it enhances. All spells of a particular technique tree +1 [[Caster Level|CL]]. 1/day it can reduce the mana cost of such a spell by 1.

The standard staff is a 1h crush weapon.
Also comes in a battle staff and light variant, like most staves.
