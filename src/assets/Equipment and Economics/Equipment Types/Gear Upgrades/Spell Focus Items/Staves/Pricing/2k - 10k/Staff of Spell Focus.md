## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">3,000g</span>

This staff has a mana crystal affixed to its end. The wood the staff is crafted from and the patterns etched into it influences what [[Technique Rules|technique]] tree it enhances. All spells of a particular technique tree have +1 [[Caster Level|CL]] while you wield this staff. 1/day it can reduce the mana cost of such a spell by 1.

See [[staff]] for base stats.
