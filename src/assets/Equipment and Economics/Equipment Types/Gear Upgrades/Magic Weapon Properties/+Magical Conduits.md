Have you ever gotten a cool weapon in a game and realized that it doesn't work for you because your character uses claws, or punches people or some other weirdo shit? Magical conduits seek to alleviate this pain by allowing you to stick with a particular theme without interfering with the mechanical benefits of looting. 

[[Unarmed Attack|Unarmed attacks]] and [[Natural Attack|natural attacks]] are a core part of fantasy, but RPG progression typically involves loot, which usually puts it in conflict with these forms of attacks that do not use weapons. If I had a dollar for every time unarmed strikes were excluded from an effect in Baldur's Gate 3...The solution is generally to give them their entirely unique section of loot, like some type of gauntlet, ki ring or what have you. This usually results in dissatisfying small selections of upgrade choices, or having to completely abandon the theme you were going for in order to play more optimally. <span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Conduits</span> are the system that I hope to alleviate this issue with.
## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">Conduit Basics</span>
By performing a brief (1 minute) and common ritual and bonding to a particular weapon requiring contact and focus, you can essentially "absorb" the qualities (including [[materials]]) of that weapon and use them with non-traditional attack forms. This includes the aforementioned natural and unarmed attacks, but also other forms of attack that break the mold like the [[Bender]] elemental blast, or whatever you cook up as a [[Mechanic]]. When you establish this bond, it is called a <span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Conduit</span>, and is contingent on your continued possession of the weapon. You can typically only establish a conduit with a singular weapon.

<span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Important note:</span> The intention is for these properties to only function with methods that involve [[attack roll]]s, but does not include [[Spell Attack|spell attacks]].

For balance reasons you simply cannot allow every single weapon in the game to copy and paste its features onto these unusual attacks. This is a can of worms I'm certain I would not be able to balance properly. For this reason, you must choose a weapon "<span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 240)">style</span>" you want to most closely emulate from the following categories.
- <span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 240)">Light weapons</span>
- <span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 240)">One-handed (1h) weapons</span>
- <span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 240)">Two-handed (2h) weapons</span>
- <span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 240)">Ranged Weapons</span> (with [[Reload]] like [[Gun|guns]])
- <span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 240)">Ranged Weapons</span> (without [[Reload]])
- <span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 240)">Exotic Weapons</span>
Obviously if you have a gun for your mech as a [[Pilot]], ranged weapons are an obvious choice, but unarmed strikes and natural attacks can be a bit weird. They are [[Light Weapon|light weapons]], but you might not always use [[DEX]] to attack with them, making it feel like a weird fit. As such, you must instead choose one such category your attacks count as. When you do this you "lock in" that particular type and it is not something you can change later when you find a new cool weapon (see the retraining section of [[+Downtime]]).

Even if you don't have say, a [[Nosey Knife]], you could still use magical conduits to give your unarmed attacks the weapon qualities found in [[+Weapons]] (or associated subsections like [[CM]]). Because of the weirdness it creates, or for balance reasons, the following traits cannot be transferred with Conduits: [[Ammunition Weapon]], [[Chink Magnet]], [[Conceal]], [[Thrown Weapon]], [[Strings Attached]], [[One and Done]], [[Improved Reach]], [[Attached]], [[Reload]], [[Short Range]], [[Long Range]], [[Versatile]]. You also do not gain a [[Staff Component|spell focus]]'s ability to cast spells, or any [[+Exotic Weapons]]' unique traits.

While a weapon is a <span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Conduit</span>, your preferred method(s) of attack is enhanced with the conduits powers. Conduits cannot enhance other [[Weapon Attack|weapon attacks]]. Your conduit loses its magic properties after it has been separated from you for about a minute. There is some form of visual representation to denote the augmented attack, like your enhanced fists taking on a red metallic sheen if you use [[Dragon Metal]], or an enhanced bite making your tongue have an eye on it if you used an [[Eye of Moogen]].
