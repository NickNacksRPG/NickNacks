## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">5,000g</span>

Tags: [[Healing]]
*This incredibly thick belt has a buckle that ends in a large fist.*

With a click of the belt buckle, you can grow to large size as a swift action. When you do so, you can also choose to spend any amount of [[mana]] to gain thrice that amount as [[Temp HP]]. You can return back to normal size as a swift action.
