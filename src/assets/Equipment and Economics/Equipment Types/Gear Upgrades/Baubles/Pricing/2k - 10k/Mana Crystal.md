## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1,500g+</span>

*Places inside of necklaces, arm bands or in more exotic forms, mana crystals are worn by mages of all kinds to empower their magic.*

Tags: [[Mana Burn]]
When equipped, you mana burn and increase your [[Caster Level|CL]]. The amount varies depending on how expensive the mana crystal is.

1,500g - Mana burn 2, +1 CL
14,000g - Mana burn 5, +2 CL
80,000g - Mana burn 9, +3 CL

Unequipping the crystal does not restore any burned mana, but it does reduce your CL. Mana crystals must be exposed to open air in order to function properly, and thus do not function when wrapped in clothing/armor or something similar.

You can also simply hold mana crystals, making them function as [[Wieldables]] (though they are not a kind of [[Staff Component|spell focus]]).
