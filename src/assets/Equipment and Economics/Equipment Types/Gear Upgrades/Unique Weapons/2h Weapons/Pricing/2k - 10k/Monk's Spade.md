## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">6,000g</span>

*This long staff has a spaded blade on one end, and a crescent blade on the other*

- Functions as a [[Tools|tool]], allowing you to dig.
- [[Weapon Attack|Weapon attacks]] made with this weapon can substitute a requirement for [[Unarmed Attack|unarmed strikes]], such as from using [[unarmed]] techniques or a [[Martial Artist]] multiattack. When doing so, its damage dice matches your unarmed damage as though any hands spent holding this weapon were empty. You do not benefit from any [[+Magical Conduits]] affecting your unarmed strikes. You still cannot [[Dual Wielding|dual wield]] with such weapons.
- When you [[defeat]] a creature of your size or smaller with this weapon, you can spend an immediate action to partially bury them in place, causing them to become [[entangled]].
- You get a +4 bonus to attack when you [[prepare]] to attack a creature you do not currently threaten. You must deal slash damage when you do this.
- Get the following [[special attack]]
<span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">Dead to Rites</span>: [[On-hit]] against an undead creature, entangle it and seal it in the earth with a paper seal. When the seal is removed (swift), the undead can escape its hole as a free action. The targeted undead cannot remove the seal.

The spade is a 2h crush and slash weapon with the [[Improved Reach]] quality.
