## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">40,000g</span> 

Tags: [[Su]], [[Air]]
*Forged by [[Cloud Giant]]s, these blades ensure you're always on the move.*

- You have [[advantage]] on any [[+Saving Throw|save]] that would reduce your movement (like inflicting [[Knocked Down|knockdown]]), or decide it for you (like [[Command]])
- Your speed is increased by 10
- You are constantly treated as though under the effects of [[Break Free]]
- Gain the following [[Special Attack|special attacks]]
<span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">Vortex</span>: Make a [[Melee Attack]]. Pull in all creatures within 20 ft of the target of your attack such that they are pulled adjacent to your target (fort reduces to 20 [[poise]] damage).
<span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">Vacuum Slice</span>: Make a [[melee attack]] with extreme speed, giving it a +4 bonus. Air rushes in to the area where you made your attack, immediately pushing all creatures in a 30 ft line (originating from and including your target) 15 ft back (fort halves to 20 poise damage).

The Zephyr has 3 [[+Magic Weapon Properties|enchantment slots]]

It is a 2h slash weapon with the [[CM]] (grapple) property and the [[Unique Special Attacks|determination]] special attack. It also has the [[Finesse]] property.
