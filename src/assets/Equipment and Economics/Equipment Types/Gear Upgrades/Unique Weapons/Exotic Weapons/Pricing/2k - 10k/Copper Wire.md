## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">8,800g</span> 

This [[Whip]] automatically comes with the [[Elemental]] (zap) enchantment, and has one additional [[+Magic Weapon Properties|enchantment slot]].

1/turn you can attack an allied [[Constructs|construct]], not to deal any damage, but to allow them to make a [[simple attack]]. If they are unable to make an attack immediately, such as no enemies being within their [[reach]] or they are [[dazed]], this has no effect.
