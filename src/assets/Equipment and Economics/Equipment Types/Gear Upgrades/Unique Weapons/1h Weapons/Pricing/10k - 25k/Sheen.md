## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">14,000g</span>

*This sword is infused with mana and shines a deep blue after its wielder taps into its mana pool by casting a spell.*

- Your [[mana]] pool increases by 1
- Whenever you cast a [[spell]], your next [[Weapon Attack]] with Sheen does an additional d10 damage. This can only happen once each round.
- Whenever you make a weapon attack with Sheen, you increase the [[Caster Level|CL]] by 1 until after you cast a spell (does not stack).

The sword is a 1h slash and pierce weapon with the [[One and Done]] quality.
