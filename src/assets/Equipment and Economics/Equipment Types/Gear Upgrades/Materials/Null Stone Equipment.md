[[Null Stone]] is a type of naturally occurring crystal that naturally grows in cave mountains. It has natural anti-magic properties that make it quite well as a material to enhance steel.

<span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Each piece of null stone equipment on your person (including those not equipped) halves your natural</span> [[mana]] <span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">regeneration (ie 1 piece makes you regenerate 1 mana every 2 rounds out of combat, 2 pieces make you only regenerate 1 mana every 4 rounds etc.)</span>

<span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">Armor/Shields (10,000)g</span> - Each piece grants you 1 [[Magic Resist]]
<span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">Weapon (5,000g)</span> - Get a +2 bonus to attack with this weapon when attempting an effect that [[Dispel|dispels]]. Gain the [[Unique Special Attacks|magic crash]] special attack, usable at-will with this weapon.
