## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1,500g</span>

Tags: [[Healing]]
*Dungle is a smith known for creating gear for young warriors and mages alike to help keep them alive. This simple wooden shield is a cornerstone of that idea.*

Whenever you use a [[+Shield Actions|shield action]], restore 1 [[+HP]].

<span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Note - </span>
Because immediate/instant actions happen before attacks, you could not heal off an [[On-hit]] [[Bleeding]] effect, or come back from defeat after resisting damage with [[Shields Up]].
