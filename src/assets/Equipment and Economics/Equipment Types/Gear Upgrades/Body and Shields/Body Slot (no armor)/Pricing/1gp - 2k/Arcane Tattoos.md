## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">250g+</span>

*A series of complex tattoos along the arms contain the components to cast a specific [[spell]].*

Choose a single spell you can memorize to inscribe on your body. You permanently memorize this spell without spending [[memory]]. You can cast this spell without a spell focus. The tattoo must be visible in order for you to benefit from this effect, meaning you cannot wear anything else in the body slot if you want to cast the spell.

The cost of the spell is based on its level. 250*(Spell LvL)^2

Level 2 - 1k

Level 4 - 4k
Level 5 - 6,259g
