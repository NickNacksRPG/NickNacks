## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">4,000g</span>

Tags: [[Su]], [[Glamer]]

*A perfectly normal cloak that renders the wearer transparent the moment that the hood comes up. The interior of the cloak reveals the runes giving the cloak its magical camouflaging properties.*

The wearer of this cloak can spend a standard action to use one hand to hold the hood on this cloak to gain [[Camouflage]] indefinitely. This effect lingers for 1 round after the hand is taken off the hood, allowing the wearer to perform brief tasks that require 2 hands, like climbing, or performing a [[DD|Surprise]] attack.
