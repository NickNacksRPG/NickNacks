## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">4,000g</span>

Tags: -
*This plain gray cloak sounds like a pile of crashing rocks when flourished.*

- When you [[Crit]], you can choose to push the creature you critically struck 10 ft away from you instead of dealing bonus damage.
- You can always reach into the cloak and pull out a small rock you can easily throw, functioning as a light [[Improvised Weapons|improvised weapon]] with the [[Ammunition Weapon]] property.
- You can [[Hide in Plain Sight]] in any rocky terrain, including worked stone in a castle
