## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">800g</span>

Tags: -

*This smelly shirt seems to be made partially out of rat hide. Perhaps it was cured in sewage?*

This smell makes any creature that wears it is persistently [[sickened]] 1. When you [[grapple]] a creature while wearing this shirt, they become sickened 1. In either case, the sickened cannot be removed without a proper wash.

Also grants the wearer [[Resistance]] to poison damage.

Outside of its mechanical benefits and drawbacks, the shirt also persistently reeks, making interactions with NPCs usually very brief while they escape your stench.
