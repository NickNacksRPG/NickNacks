## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">1,000g</span>

Tags: -

*A snazzy hat with a rat on it.*

- This hat allows the creature to communicate with all rats and similar rodent-like creatures, as though under the effects of [[Speak with Animals]] and [[Tongues]].
- You can use [[+Persuasion]] to gain rats as permanent companions (DC 15+rat's [[LvL]]). You get a +4 bonus to such checks.
- Rats will never attack you unless you attack them first, or otherwise threaten them.
- 1/day you can procure a small piece of cheese from underneath the hat.
