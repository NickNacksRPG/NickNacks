## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">6,000g</span>

Tags: -

_This mask creates an air-tight seal around the face that filters the air you breathe through a small compartment filled with common herbs. The most common type includes a bird-like motif._

- You have [[Immunity]] to the effects of [[Toxic Gas]], unless it is so dense that you cannot see through it.
- You have [[Advantage]] on saves against effects that inflict some kind of disease
- You have advantage on saves against any other form of harmful gaseous effects (like [[Euphoric Cloud]] or [[Nauseating Trail]])
