*This ghostly blue hood is made using the remnants of ectoplasm left behind by defeated ghosts as a sort of dye while weaving the fabric.*

This hood allows you to see [[Incorporeal]] creatures while they hiding through a surface that you can see. This basically means that you can see a ghost through a single wall in a house, but not an entire house.
