You have a connection to a particularly wealthy individual who enables your particularly lavish lifestyle. Your friend is well renowned for their hedonistic tendencies to such an extent that merely being associated with them is enough to carry you through life.

You have what essentially amounts to a credit card: A tab that you can use in any establishment that sells superfluous niceties such as drinks, excessively expensive lodging, or to arrange house parties. The seal on your card carries so much weight that you can use it to enter exclusive clubs for the wealthy, or as a pass to get into parties when you aren't on the guest list.

There are still limitations on what your connection can provide for you. For example, if you try to join a party to an opposing faction, you will likely be apprehended immediately. If you are in the middle of the wilderness, you will not get a chauffeur to deliver you a special bottle of wine. The benefits of this background can only be experienced while within civilized society, and within your connection's sphere of influence, or at least where people are neutral towards your contact. 

Generally speaking, the intention of this background is to provide the player with basically unlimited money to spend on things that<span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)"> don't have a particular mechanical benefit</span>. Every night you can drink expensive drinks, but not if they are a [[+Potions|potion]]. You can ride exotic creatures for fun, but you cant walk away with a dragon mount.
