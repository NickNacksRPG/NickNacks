Very simply, here! The mortal realm is the realm that includes all of the places in Nack (the planet) and the planets, stars, etc outside of its atmosphere. It is called the mortal realm because there are many immortal (aka without a biological time limit) creatures that inhabit other realms, like [[Celestials]] and [[Fiends]].

Most adventures take place in the mortal realm, and more specifically, on the planet of Nack.
