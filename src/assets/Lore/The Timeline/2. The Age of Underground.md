TL;DR
The age of underground lasted around 200 years. Several [[Dark Elf]] societies simultaneously began a hostile takeover over an otherwise peaceful world filled with dragons. [[Dark Iron Dwarves]] were instrumental in undermining the alliances between [[The Dragon Aspects]]. The age was plagued by constant war and suffering that eventually came to a close when nearly all subterranean races were banished to [[The Shadow Realm]].

<span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">Dawn of a New Age</span>
The turn of the age to the Underground was marked by the 3 dark elven queens, [[Shi'Sulm]], [[Shi'Zen]] and [[Shi'Sara]] leading a coordinated attack on major population centers around the world, both for dragons and man. This coincided with a betrayal of one of [[The Dragon Aspects]], [[T'zarborn]], and his corruption by the hands of the [[Dark Iron Dwarves]]. One clan in particular, [[The Blackwing Clan]], did not hunt down dragons as many of the neighboring clans did, instead opting to accept the aid and companionship of T'zarborn. A sinister plot was in the making to pull T'zarborn away from his fellow dragon aspects and fill his head with grandiose ambitions. 

This was a plan that would take time, and the Blackwing Clan was extremely patient. They offered him aid in any way they could, such as to take care of his children. This service became more and more important in providing them with a safe haven in a world that was becoming increasingly hostile to dragon-kind over the centuries. One child in particular, [[T'zarzenite]], was held in regard high above the rest for her peculiar talent in producing quantities of offspring expected from dozens of her kind. For this she was given the title "Broodmother." She was practically worshipped as much as her father was by the Blackwings.

As the [[1. The Age of Dragons|The Age of Dragons]] entered its 5th century, the Blackwing Clan's plan entered its final stage. Previous alliances crumbled one by one, and T'zarborn had little else to turn to that his still loyal friends in the Blackwings. It was now they finally decided to remind him of his importance in defeating [[Drakthar, the World Ender]], and how he was the mightiest of the dragon aspects. The old ways were failing, and it was time to usher in a new age. One with him at the top. They were able to manipulate him into forcing T'zarzenite to bear an army practically all on her own so that way he could take control of the world. Soon he would lead the [[Dark Iron Empire]].

T'zarborn and the Blackwings first started with [[Suirei]]. The first to be betrayed, she was also the last one that was successfully captured before the rest of the Dragon Aspects prepared accordingly. Eventually the armies of the Blackwings overwhelming the mostly demilitarized societies. The Aspects went into hiding, sealing themselves away in obscure locations alongside their most dedicated followers. It would be up to humanoids to solve the problems of this era.

<span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">This war went on for many decades.</span> Land would change hands between dark elves, elves, dark iron dwarves and dwarves. Other races were not spared the suffering. T'zarborn called forth an entirely new elite group of [[Elementals]] he named after himself known as the [[T'zar]]. The T'zar cared little for politics, and were happy to battle whoever, making them a very volatile arm of his military, but few were able to match up to their martial prowess. 

The T'zar turned out to be a chink in the seemingly impenetrable armor of the new world order. The Elves and Dwarves independently came up with similar strategies, feigning a huge battle before pulling back and boxing the opposing army into battle with the T'zar. This tactic was successful for the elves, allowing them to press the advantage and take back much of their territory. The dwarves were a bit late in developing this strategy, and so by then the Dark Iron dwarves had developed contingency plans. Dwarven society collapsed and the only ones that lived were those that fled to the far reaches of the world.

With the foothold the elves managed to get, they pooled all of their resources to once again use their advanced sealing magic to prepare another trap. In a coordinated strike in several key battles, many elves sacrificed themselves in order to activate a great sealing ritual that banished nearly the vast majority of dark elven armies to [[The Shadow Realm]]. This caused dark elves to practically vanish overnight as those they oppressed killed their masters through sheer numbers.

With the dark elves out of the picture, suddenly it laid entirely on the Dark Iron dwarves to rule the entire world, as task they were ill equipped to handle when thrust upon them so suddenly.  Independently across the world, other groups began to revolt against their Dark Iron overlords. The Broodmother was hunted down and captured. T'zarborn chased after his daughter and was later captured due to his recklessness. With so many of the dark elves gone and their constant supply of draconic goons eliminated, the Dark Iron empire buckled under the pressure. Soon even lowly bandits were able to cause major problems when resupplying troops or travelling the countryside. Eventually the Blackwing Clan disappeared entirely. Dark Iron cities were ransacked and any foolish enough not to flee were killed. The T'zar, once they lost the intelligence and coordination of their masters, were trapped and sealed away after another group of elven mages and dragons sacrificed themselves to perform the ritual, this time sealing them deep underground beneath the planet's crust.

<span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">What followed was another age of peace.</span> The newly freed societies were now left with the task of deciding who got to live where. Not everybody got the land they wanted since entirely new lands were formed from the smaller groups that fought against the Dark Iron Empire. While humans were the majority race worldwide, not all humans thought the same way. Local heroes became the leaders of new countries like [[Dragon's Den]], [[The Land of the Flying Boar]] and [[The Land of the Weebs]]. The most unified group of humans lived within [[The Land of Fire]]. Many of the smaller leaders that got their own countries willingly ceded that control in order to form what is now known as [[The Flame Empire]]. 

For the remaining decades of the age, things were relatively peaceful with only minor regional conflicts popping up. Occasionally a small group of Dark Elves were found that caused trouble. In particular, a group of tightly knit bandits known as [[The Ravens]] continued to cause trouble all along the western coast of [[The Land of Earth]].
