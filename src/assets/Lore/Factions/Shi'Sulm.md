The Shi'Sulm dark elves lived primarily in an underground swamp region in heart of [[The Land of Earth]]. Like most dark elves, they came to prominence during the end of [[1. The Age of Dragons|The Age of Dragons]], and dominated the world during [[2. The Age of Underground|The Age of Underground]]. During [[4. The Age of Darkness|The Age of Darkness]], they emerged from their prison in [[The Shadow Realm]] in order to wreak havoc once again. The Shi'Sulm typically have brown skin and silver hair. They universally have silver eyes. Many have insectoid parts like antennae or chitin as well.

The Shi'Sulm are led by a royal family of matriarchs by the same name and are actually an offshoot of the [[Shi'Zen]] that wanted a much more open society and migrated northwest. The Shi'Sulm royal family are somehow reviled even more than their sister tribes due to the strange relationship they have with insects: They figured out ways interbreed with insects, creating mostly elven insectoid hybrids. Through this process, they were able to create all kinds of new insects, many of which possessed high degrees of intelligence. This interbreeding is exclusively the right of the royal family. In fact, nobody other than the royal family can even reproduce at all. The control they possess over reproduction creates a large degree of uniformity in the genetic makeup of the population. The royal family is capable of communicating to any member of their [[Hive Mind]] telepathically, and to their entire family with vague psychic impressions communicating strong emotions like fear or anger.

The Shi'Sulm are also reviled for their spies, both the insects themselves and the elves that shapeshift into them. What was originally an annoying fly in your room can suddenly become a life or death situation to get rid of. The Shi'Sulm are extremely xenophobic, and are always on the lookout for nearby civilizations that seek to destroy them. Their paranoia pushes them to preemptively strike using swarms of insects to deliver deadly diseases, devour crops and poison food supplies. Their mastery over pests turns life into a living hell for any that are unlucky enough to settle near their sphere of influence.

Overall, Shi'Sulm society is very different from their sister families. Because they use insects in order to propagate, the Shi'Sulm completely circumvent the need for any male elves at all, and are thus always female. This eliminates a major point of tension that exists in other societies that allows for much greater degrees of cooperation. Left to their own devices, the Shi'Sulm are isolationist and are happy to live in their own world separate from all other peoples. Unfortunately, the expansionist nature of humanoids, and in particular [[human]] civilization always inevitably disturbs this peace and causes conflicts that rarely end well for those that intrude. The Shi'Sulm worship the [[Earth Mother]] to a fault, preferring an extreme militant approach to any that intrude upon the land she blessed them with.
