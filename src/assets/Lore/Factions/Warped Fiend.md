Warped fiends are only plentiful in circles that heavily align themselves with [[Dark Elf]] groups and those interested in [[The Shadow Realm]]. The practices of these fiends take the [[Shi'Sara]] practice of [[Fleshwarping]] in a very particular direction mostly focused on self improvement. They torture *themselves* as a form of "training" to improve themselves. The method they choose to do so varies from creature to creature, but it usually involves some of the most painful things imaginable like having piercings all over the body with heavy chains, freezing themselves solid or cutting themselves. 

These violent tendencies extend to others, naturally, whom they want to also improve with these special training regiments. They also are happy to kill other creatures to steal their favorite parts, performing elaborate surgeries to wear their skin, or attach the extracted limbs to their body.
