Fleshwarping is [[Dark Elf]] [[Alchemy]] mixed with [[+Medicine]] used to forcefully change creatures into monstrous versions of themselves. The resulting creatures are usually mentally scarred so badly from the process that they have no will left of their own and as such, are subservient to their creator. What exactly the creature is transformed into through the process is based on what species it is, with the process usually transforming creatures into similar types if they are from the same [[+Races|race]] for instance.

The process itself is usually incredibly painful involving completely reshaping the body while the patient is still kept alive. Subjects are bitten by vats of aquatic spiders submerged in toxic liquids, they are injected with chemically infused blood that burns them from the inside out and all manner of creature methods that could be mistaken for torture to the untrained eye.

Dark elves themselves use fleshwarping to transform themselves in to centaur-like spidery creatures with elven bodies attached called Driders. Most that do so are part of the [[Shi'Sara]] dark elven family, or have their family lineage tied to them.
