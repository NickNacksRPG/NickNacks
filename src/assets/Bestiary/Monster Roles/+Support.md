This monster specializes in aiding their allies, typically either by healing or preventing damage. Support characters work well when paired with a [[+Bruiser]], who is often going to be taking many hits. Supports do well against [[AoE]] if they have healing, or high damage dealers if they prevent damage. Support characters are weak to surprise attacks, since [[DD]] makes them unable to use their reactive defensive features.
