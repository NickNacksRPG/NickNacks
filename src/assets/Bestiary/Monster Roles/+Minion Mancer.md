This monster specializes in commanding allies and generating new allies on the spot. They get an enormous amount of value from [[+Teamwork Feats]] and often have ways to leverage these minions in unique ways. They are very weak to [[AoE]], as such effects tend to get their damage multiplied by the numerous minions in play.
