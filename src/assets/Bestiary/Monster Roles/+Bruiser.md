The most common type of creature.

This monster specializes in taking hits and dealing them back. They will often have high base stats and a pretty simple game plan of run into the middle of the battle and deal damage. They are self-sufficient enemies, though they will suffer if they are unable to reach their enemies due to superior range or mobility.
