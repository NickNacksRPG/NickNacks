This monster specializes in ranged combat. Their primary objective is to deal consistent damage while staying at a safe distance. They frequently use [[Partial Cover]] and [[+Stealth]] to great effect. They excel against squishy backline characters and characters with low mobility. Creatures that use [[+Shield|shields]] or with high mobility can give marksman a lot of trouble.
