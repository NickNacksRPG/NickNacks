This monster specializes in controlling the flow of battle, typically via creating terrain, and [[persistent]] effects that restrict movement or methods of attack. They often focus on inflicting [[+Status|status effects]] over dealing damage. Controllers are powerful when paired with a [[+Bruiser]] or an [[+Infiltrator]]. Controllers are weak against creatures with high enough mobility to navigate their hazards, [[healing]] effects that negate their status or enough damage to dispatch allied damage dealers making the hazards and [[CC]] the controller provides less valuable.
