This monster specializes in isolating and dispatching key targets, catching players by surprise and gathering information before the fight starts. Typically via [[+Stealth]] or [[Hide in Plain Sight]]. They can often create ideal hiding conditions via [[Light Level|Darkness]] or some kind of [[Fog]]. They have high mobility, letting them get to where they need to be to finish off low health enemies or hit squishy backline characters. They do poorly when they are exposed before they sneak attack, or when their plan is interrupted by [[CC]].
