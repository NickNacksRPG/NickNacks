<span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">Battle Tactics -</span> 
Void puppies excel when left alone and allowed to hammer down on the same target over and over again over multiple turns. As such, they are excellent with [[High Ground]] and some [[Partial Cover|cover]] to keep them safe from counterattacks. Their only defensive features are [[Blood Spurt]] and [[Shot From the Air]], making them very vulnerable to being [[DD]], [[Martial Artist]]s and [[+Combat Maneuver|combat maneuvers]].

[[+Controller]] creatures pair excellently with void puppies, particularly those specialized in [[+Combat Maneuver|combat maneuvers]] since they can easily lower a creature's [[AC]]. Combat maneuvers can also keep enemies at bay with things like [[grapple]]. Void puppies also [[threaten]] in melee thanks to [[Snap Shot]], letting them capitalize on [[Maneuver Master]]'s bonus effect if an ally [[drag]]s an assassin away.

The void puppy's feat selection makes it a surprisingly self sufficient ranged attacker, giving it the ability to fight back in melee and immediately create more distance thanks to the combination of [[Point Blank Master]] and [[Fleet Footwork]].
