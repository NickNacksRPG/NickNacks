Dragons are a distinct set of reptilian creature that usually sets itself apart from other beasts through sentience, size and propensity for magic. Dragons also usually have special glands in their throats they can use to launch elemental [[breath weapon]]s. More about the history of dragons can be read in [[1. The Age of Dragons|The Age of Dragons]]. 

Dragons can breed with other types of creatures, almost always leading to dragons similar to the mother. They can lay eggs frequently and in large numbers. But if this were the case with no other factors, they surely the world would quickly be overtaken by [[Brood Mother]]s. Dragons express [polyphenism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyphenism), where many dragon children do not grow past the [[Whelp]] stage. The exact environmental circumstances required for a dragon to fully mature are not well understood, even by dragon kind themselves. Many ambitious dragons try to crack the code to create an army of powerful creatures.
