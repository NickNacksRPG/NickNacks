## <span style="color:rgb(247, 138, 255)">Weaknesses/Deep Lore</span>

<span style="color:rgb(247, 138, 255)">Boss Mechanic: Commandments</span> - At the start of each round of combat (minus the first turn if [[DD|surprised]]), the Sovereign decrees one of the following commandments. All creatures that can see/hear or otherwise sense them must follow the commandment. If they do not, they take 20 holy damage and are subject to divine punishment (no save). A sovereign cannot make the same commandment in consecutive rounds.
