<span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 240)">Parasite</span> [[Su]] - As a swift action, a detached yummi can attach to a creature within 10 feet, gently floating around them at a fixed distance in its space. It acts in tandem with the creature in initiative, using its actions interchangeably with theirs. It cannot be subject to any effects and is [[Untargetable]], even to [[AoE]] effects, while attached. If the attached creature is [[defeated]], yummi is [[stunned]] for 1 round and detaches. A yummi can detach as a standard action, popping out in an adjacent space. 

After being attached for 3 rounds and performing no action, a yummi is [[Invisible]] until it attacks, uses a technique without the [[Subtle]] tag, or uses its Magic Book feature. It cannot be detected if the creature it is attached to cant be detected unless the creature it is attached to can be detected, such as if it is [[Invisible]] or is using [[Hide in Plain Sight]].

<span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Total Control (Su)</span> [[Possessed|possession]] - A yummi attached to a creature with its Parasite feature can take control of its host as a standard action (will negates). The yummi must spend its own actions and the creature's actions to command the creature, meaning the first turn of possession is pretty weak. If the possession is successful, the possessed creature cannot take any actions on its turn. 
## <span style="color:rgb(247, 138, 255)">Weaknesses/Deep Lore</span>

<span style="color:rgb(247, 138, 255)">Just a Myth</span> - When a yummi is defeated, it gets sucked into its magic book permanently. With a DC 25 [[arcana]] check it can be summoned again with a 1 minute ritual at 1 hp. If the book is destroyed, the yummi is killed. A caster can cast any spells the yummi can innately cast using this book when it is not summoned, as though it was a [[Tome of Knowledge (T1)]]. Each non-cantrip technique requires 2 [[memory]]. A creature must still be a high enough level to use the techniques (see [[Technique Progression]]).
<span style="color:rgb(247, 138, 255)">Helpless Baby</span> - A yummi is always considered a [[Minion]]. 
<span style="color:rgb(247, 138, 255)">GET IT OFF ME</span> - When the yummi is attached via its Parasite feature, all instances of [[Forced Movement]] deal [[poise]] damage to the yummi equal to the distance it is moved. A creature that does not want a yummi attached to it can choose to [[+Combat Maneuver|combat maneuver]] it directly. When poise broken, a yummi automatically detaches and becomes targetable again. It cannot use its parasite feature on its next turn.
