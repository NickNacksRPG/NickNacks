Battle Tactics - 
Plague golems primarily exist to completely demolish martial characters that cannot quickly whittle them down. Once a [[Fighter]] gets a few sickened stacks, the plague golem's shield makes it practically impossible to hurt it or its allies with direct attacks. As such they will usually focus on closing the gap against anybody they see with weapons drawn or that are otherwise attacking quite often.
