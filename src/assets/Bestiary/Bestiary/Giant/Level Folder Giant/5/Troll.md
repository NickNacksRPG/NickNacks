<span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Hunger </span>[[Fear]] - A troll can spend a standard action to stare at its next meal. The creature is [[frightened]] for 3 rounds (will negates). Frightened creatures get a new save each turn. Any "meal" is susceptible to a trolls unique special attacks, regardless if it is frightened or not.
<span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Spice Up</span>: [[Special Attack]] - Make a [[Dirty Trick]] against a meal with advantage. The creature is [[Blinded]] and [[Sickened]] 2 until removed. Each condition can be removed with a standard action by wiping away the "spices."
<span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Tenderize</span> - Make 3 [[Melee Attack|melee attacks]] against a meal. One attack must be a [[Topple]].
<span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Rock Collector </span>- Trolls love rocks of all shapes and sizes. Their rock attacks can deal crush, slash or pierce damage. Roll a [[Luck Check]] when making troll encounters to see if they have rare rocks. A 10 grants them one rock, and a 20 gets two. A rare rock can deal elemental damage, or have functionality similar to [[+Thrown Concoctions]]. Get creative!
## <span style="color:rgb(247, 138, 255)">Weaknesses/Deep Lore</span>

<span style="color:rgb(247, 138, 255)">Meat Lover</span> - If a meal presents a large quantity of meat, comparable to its own size (even illusory if the troll fails to save), the troll is dazed for one round as it eats this new meat. It cannot use its Hunger feature after this, nor does it get baited by further meat. The creature loses its meal status.
<span style="color:rgb(247, 138, 255)">Oily</span> - With a successful [[+Survival]] check, enough of the trolls natural oils can be extracted from a willing or defeated troll to create a [[Trollblood Potion]].
<span style="color:rgb(247, 138, 255)">Shinies</span> - Trolls love shiny rocks. When looting trolls, roll a luck check. They possess 100*(luck roll) gold in shiny rocks.
