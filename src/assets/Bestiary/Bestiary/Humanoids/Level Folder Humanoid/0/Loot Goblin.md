## <span style="color:rgb(247, 138, 255)">Weaknesses/Deep Lore</span>

<span style="color:rgb(247, 138, 255)">Shinies</span> - A creature can wave around a valuable object to tantalize a loot goblin. A valuable enough object taunts the goblin, forcing it to steal the item (will negates). The object must have at least a 1k gold value. If it is worth at least 10k, the goblin gets no save.

A pouch of gold is satisfactory as well. Once presented, the item can be the target of the loot goblin's "claimed" ability.
