<span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Battle Tactics</span> 
Amerin is expected to fight along side [[Giant Rat]] and [[Rat Swarm]] enemies, with a larger number of singular rats than swarms. The number of which should vary depending on party size. With 4 player characters 3 swarms and 6 giant rats is a substantial challenge.

Before the battle, Amerin will gather information about his foes with his Little Beady Eyes feature. He might even participate in other battles leading up to him via his [[MM Caster Minion]] feat.

Once in battle, Amerin will always try to keep his distance from enemies, using allied rats via [[Escape Route]] to reposition to safety. Open up with a [[Toxic Waste]] to better enable the rats to inflict plague. Use [[Gust]] to push people into it. Because of Amerin's resistance to poison and immunity to sickened, he can even opt to stand in the toxic waste to evade melee characters, albeit while suffering persistent damage and limiting his access to rats for his boss actions.
