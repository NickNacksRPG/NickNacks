## <span style="color:rgb(247, 138, 255)">Weaknesses/Deep Lore</span>

<span style="color:rgb(247, 138, 255)">L O N G</span> - While a giant anaconda is a massive creature, its body is an unusual shape. Therefore it's head and body should be represented in 2 different ways. The head as a traditional token and the body more like a terrain hazard. An anaconda only [[Threaten|threatens]] with its head. They body should be represented as a 60 ft line that is 10 ft wide (and tall) that can twist and turn around terrain (I recommend using a string or a toy as a physical token).  
Creatures can scale the side of a giant anaconda with a DC 10 athletics check, and they can balance on top of it as it moves with a DC 15 [[balance]] check.  
Any part of the snake can be attacked, and the snake has a [[Reach|natural reach]] of 20 ft.
