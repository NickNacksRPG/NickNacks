## <span style="color:rgb(247, 138, 255)">Weaknesses/Deep Lore</span>

<span style="color:rgb(247, 138, 255)">Boss Mechanic: Phases</span> - Calamitas has 3 "phases," with each after the first giving her access to new more powerful abilities. In order to advance to the next phase, her summons must be defeated. Abilities that require being in a specific phase are marked appropriately (ie P3 for phase 3). With every phase transition she also spawns 6 [[Tortured Soul]]s whose DC increase to 25. The big summons act immediately, but the tortured souls act at the end of initiative.

Her first major summon is [[ligma]]
Her second major summon is [[sugma]]
