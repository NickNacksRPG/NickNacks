## <span style="color:rgb(247, 138, 255)">Weaknesses/Deep Lore</span>

<span style="color:rgb(247, 138, 255)">L O N G</span> - While Sepulcher is a massive creature, its body is an unusual shape. Therefore it's head and body should be represented in 2 different ways. The head as a traditional token and the body more like a terrain hazard. It only [[Threaten|threatens]] with its head. The body should be represented as a 60 ft line that is 10 ft wide (and tall) that can twist and turn around terrain (I recommend using a string or a toy as a physical token).  
Creatures can scale the side of a Sepulcher with a DC 10 [[+Athletics]] check, and they can balance on top of it as it moves with a DC 15 [[balance]] check.  
Any part of the the boney snake can be attacked, and the it has a [[Reach|natural reach]] of 20 ft.
<span style="color:rgb(247, 138, 255)">Aimless Soul</span> - Sepulcher is an agent of [[+The Brimstone Witch]]. Its existence in [[Mortal Realm|The Mortal Realm]] is an attempt from the Brimstone Witch to escape from her prison. It typically wanders the world in search of powerful sources of magic.
