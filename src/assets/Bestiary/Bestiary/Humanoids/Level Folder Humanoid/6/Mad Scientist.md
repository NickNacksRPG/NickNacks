Battle Tactics - 
Mad scientists are always designed to be paired with a [[Constructs|construct]] of some kind to make use of their unique weapon, [[Copper Wire]]. In particular creatures that have powerful [[Simple Attack|simple attacks]] can benefit heavily. The unique support ability in Supercharge also only works with constructs, and is very risky, considering it can be easily disrupted, but has an enormous payoff if left unchecked for multiple rounds.

Outside of this synergy, mad scientists make good use of [[+Cantrips]] thanks to their unique multiattack. Depending which cantrip they use, they can deal decent damage with [[Acid Splash]], or push foes in precarious positions with [[Gust]]. Positioned optimally, they can do all of these things together.

Lastly, their unique defensive ability, Siphon, gets the most value with some big healthy idiots to hook up to to keep themselves alive. Alternatively, they can also use a hostage, or a sacrificial lesser minions to keep themselves healthy.
