Battle Tactics - 
Plague doctors have access to a wide array of [[Alchemical Items]], mostly [[+Toxins]]. Their highest priority is to apply as many of these items to their enemies as reliably as possible. Against distant enemies, they do so with [[+Stealth]] and their bows. Against close enemies, they prefer to [[Feint]] and jab them with knives.

Plague doctors generally avoid attacking creatures that look particularly beefy, and those that wield shields, preferring squishy backline characters or out of position. If forced to attack shielded foes, they utilize [[Catapult]] to bypass the AC check to apply their alchemical items.

If none of these tactics prove useful, the Plague Doctor will default to using their [[Arc]] techniques for support. If the they are on their last legs, they will attempt to inflict a [[Blood Price]] curse upon the enemy they have seen casting the most spells.
