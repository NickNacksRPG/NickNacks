## <span style="color:rgb(247, 138, 255)">Weaknesses/Deep Lore</span>

<span style="color:rgb(247, 138, 255)">Fluffy</span> - The owlbear possesses the rare combo of having fur and feathers, making for an exceptionally fluffy combination. If you break an owlbear's poise with a combat maneuver, you can choose to instead give the owl bear a good pet, making them [[Staggered]] for 1 round.  
Can only be performed once per combat.
<span style="color:rgb(247, 138, 255)">Cubs</span> - Owl bear mothers will often train their children to hunt from a young age. These cubs are level 1 and lack the techniques, multiattack, rage and leaping capabilities their mother has. If a cub is brought below half hp, an owlbear parent can activate their rage earlier.
