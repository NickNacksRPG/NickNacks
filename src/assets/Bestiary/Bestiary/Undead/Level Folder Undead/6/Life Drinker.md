<span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Necrotic Touch</span> [[Su]] [[Healing]] [[Necrotic]] - Instead of a traditional attack, a life drinker drains the life from its foes with melee touches, dealing necrotic damage and healing equal to half the damage dealt.
<span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Grasp of the Undying</span> - Any creature grappled by a life drinker or its hands takes 10 necrotic damage and is [[bone chilled]] for 1 turn. Can also be done as a maintain option.
<span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Handy (Su)</span> - A life drinker is surrounded by the spectral hands of the dead, and can command them to grapple their enemies with a +11 bonus (with the ability to apply effects like [[Maneuver Versatility]] and [[Maneuver Master]] as normal).

* Creatures that end their turn within [[reach]] of a life drinker are automatically subject to a [[grapple]].
* A Life drainer can summon a hand that immediately grapples a creature within 30 ft as a swift action.
* When a life drinker dies, a hand spawns in its space and adjacent to its killer, and both immediately attempt to grapple a creature

The hands act autonomously and do not impede the movement of the life drinker. They threaten, cannot move and act on the life drinkers turn. A hand cannot be killed, but by making a [[shove]] or [[drag]] attack it can be shoved back into or pulled out of the ground automatically, causing them to disappear.
<span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Grasp of the Starved</span> - Target a point within 30 ft. Make a grapple attack with a +14 bonus against all enemies within 10 ft of that point as numerous hands reach to grab them. Any creature grappled has a hand linger to maintain it while the rest recede.
