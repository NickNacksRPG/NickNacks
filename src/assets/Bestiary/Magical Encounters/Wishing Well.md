## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">Identify DC 20</span> [[Arcana]]

[[Geniekin]] and followers of [[Ulule]] get [[Advantage]] on checks to identify the effects of a wishing well and can attempt to do so without having the arcana skill. To the untrained eye, the wishing well looks like an average well. But in reality, [[Fey]] and followers of [[Frogg Saran]] will create wishing wells in order to provide themselves with entertainment, or as a slightly inconsistent method of helping mortals.

Creatures that successfully identify a wishing well are well aware of the whimsical nature of these watering holes. They know that the power of the wishing well doesn't allow the wisher to completely change reality, but rather has the power to alter circumstances surrounding the wish: Sometimes to improve the scenario, and other times to hinder it. Once the magic of a wishing well is used once, it vanishes and cannot be used again.

When tossing a single gold coin into a wishing well, a creature must roll a [[Luck Check]], with the dice roll determining the result.

If the roll is less than 7, then the wish becomes <span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">twisted</span>. 
If the roll is greater than 7, but less than 17, then the wish becomes <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">chaotic</span>.
If the roll is greater than 17, then the wish becomes <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">beneficial</span>.

<span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">Twisted</span> wishes actively create obstacles and hinder the creature making the wish. <span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">Chaotic</span> wishes don't necessarily do anything in specific to help or harm, but rather create some type of fantastical effect tangentially related to the wish. <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">Beneficial</span> wishes are always directly helpful.

Because a wish can cover a wide variety of circumstances, it will require a great deal of creativity to come up with what the effects will be on the spot. Here are some potential (but not all) outcomes of the well to get the creative juices flowing...
- Summon a creature. They can be helpful, directly harmful through a combat encounter, or covertly harmful by deceiving the party.
- Summon an item. This could mean [[+Cursed Items]], [[Gear Upgrades]], [[+Mundane Items|mundane items]] or [[Alchemical Items]]. Maybe the potions have unforeseen side effects...
- Create a portal that can [[Teleportation|teleport]] creatures to a location. Teleportation already has plenty of ways it can go wrong, such as teleporting into somewhere dangerous or in the middle of a private affair.
- Give some kind of temporary [[buff]], or inflict some type of [[curse]]
