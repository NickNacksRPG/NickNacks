Engulf is a passive ability that allows creatures to capture enemies inside of themselves, but in a way that is less effective than [[swallow]] since they are usually within reach of being saved. Common on [[Oozes]].

When the creature succeeds on a [[grapple]] check against a creature of their size or smaller, they can engulf the target. The target gains the [[entombed]] condition. Usually the creature can take no action other than to use [[Breakout]] to escape. It requires no action on the part of the creature to maintain the engulfed status and does not slow them down.

Allies can use [[breakout]] as a standard action to pull you out or poise break the creature to set you free.
