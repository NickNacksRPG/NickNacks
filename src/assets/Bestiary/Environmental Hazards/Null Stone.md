## <span style="color:rgb(103, 254, 120)">Identify DC 16</span> [[+Nature]]

Bane of all mages, null stone is a naturally occurring crystal very typical in mountain caves. It is neither particularly rare nor common. It is an opaque white crystal similar to quartz, with the telltale sign being a blue dust peppering its interior and exterior. Null stone has been used to protect locations against mages, and make stone structures completely immune to [[Earthbender]]s. They also make good equipment to battle against them. This makes it popular amongst [[Light Guardians]] and [[Arc (L)]] members.

Simply existing within 30 ft of significant deposits of this rock (roughly human-sized) is enough to protect against most magic, granting 2 [[Magic Resist]]. They also prevent natural [[mana]] regeneration. Null stone is worth its weight in gold, and takes up an [[Inventory|inventory slot]] for every 25 lbs. Carrying and transporting null stone is dangerous because it prevents the mana regeneration of those near it. These smaller deposits typically weigh a few hundred pounds.

Particularly large crystals (those as large as an elephant) also require a [[Caster Level]] check to cast any spells at all. The DC is 12 and the radius is 60 ft.  These deposits typically weigh thousands of pounds.

Enormous house-sized crystals require a DC 18 caster level check in a 120 ft radius. These deposits typically weigh many tons.
