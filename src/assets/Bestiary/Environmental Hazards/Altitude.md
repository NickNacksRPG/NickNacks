As elevation increases, air pressure decreases, leaving less oxygen in the air to breathe. When attempting to climb to high altitudes without acclimation, creatures can succumb to varying degrees of altitude sickness. Distances are measured relative to sea level.

<span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">Below 6,000 ft</span> - The effects of altitude sickness are typically pretty minor below 6,000 ft. Shortness of breath and such. Temporarily ascending to such heights causes no ill effects.
<span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">Above 6,000 ft</span> - The effects of altitude sickness start to emerge. Creatures are [[exhausted]] after existing at this elevation for a few minutes (DC 15 fort negates). For every 3,000 feet above 6,000, the DC increases by 5. Every hour that passes while traversing this terrain requires an additional save (note becoming exhausted while already exhausted inflicts [[drained]] 5).
<span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">Above 27,000 ft</span> - The death zone. Creatures are automatically drained 1 each minute they spend at this height.

Certain abilities and items that let you breathe normally can allow you resist or ignore the conditions of high altitude.
