Tags: [[Fire]], [[Persistent]]

Fire can be created by all sorts of things in the environment, including your spells!

<span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">Lesser Fires:</span> These are baby fires, born from a recent fire effect that touches moderately flammable material. We like to imagine most fires as a blazing impassible wall that immediately springs up, but if you've ever tried to start a fire, you'd know its quite difficult to go from zero to completely coated in flames. Left on its own however, a lesser fire can increase in size over time, usually over the course of minutes unless some kind of kindling or chemical ignition is used.

In combat, lesser fires have no impact. Things are on fire, but the fire is not big enough to cause any damage to creatures moving through it, though interacting with objects could potentially be painful. <span style="color:rgb(192, 0, 0)">All abilities that create fire effects are assumed to create lesser fires unless stated otherwise.</span> 

<span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">Inferno:</span> Infernos are the dangerous fires that cover nearly the entire building, forest or other oily surface. Infernos treat you as though you were [[ablaze]] during any turn you move through them. Any such turn you also roll a DC 10 reflex save or the fire clings and you become ablaze. Infernos created by a creature's ability have this DC increase to the creature's DC.

Infernos also typically involve [[smoke]] when in enclosed spaces.
